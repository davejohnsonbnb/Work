2. Our strategy in plain words

↑ Back to contents

Why start with only these two products?

  • They are ready. The platform and the survey method already exist. The marketplace and auctions still need escrow, legal set-up and partners.
  • They work together. Subscriptions bring in many people cheaply and build our name. Surveys bring in the real money. A subscriber who likes the map becomes a survey customer, and a survey customer gets a subscription to keep using the data.
  • They build proof. Every survey delivered well becomes a case study. Later, those case studies sell compliance reports, government projects and the marketplace.

The four ideas behind everything we do

IdeaWhat it means in practice
1. Sell trust, not satellitesSatellite pictures are free for anyone (NASA, Europe). Other companies already do satellite exploration, for example Fleet Space at Reko Diq and Farmonaut in India, and SUPARCO launched Pakistan's own hyperspectral satellite in 2025. Our edge is that we combine satellite analysis with Pakistan's licence records, mining law and local knowledge, and explain it in plain language, in English and Urdu.
2. Two price listsPakistani customers pay in PKR at local prices. Foreign companies and investors pay in USD at international prices, which are still below global competitors.
3. Relationships close deals, online builds volumeSurveys are sold face to face: meetings, events, introductions. That is the Business Lead's strength. Subscriptions are sold online: website, social media, WhatsApp, universities. That is the Growth Lead's strength.
4. Honesty is our brandSatellites cannot see gold, platinum or copper directly. They map the rocks those metals live in. We say this upfront. A result of “no strong target” is still valuable, because it saves the client from wasting money on fieldwork. Clients who trust us come back.

Why these prices are fair

What the AI allowance means

Each package includes a monthly amount of AI use, measured technically in “tokens”. We show customers approximate questions per month, which is easier to understand. Extra AI can be bought: PKR 1,000 / USD 5 for about 250 more questions.

5. Product 2: Satellite survey reports

↑ Back to contents

What a survey is: the client gives us the boundary of their land (a licence area or a block they're interested in). Our analysts study it using several satellites. The report tells the client which parts of the land show the rock types and alteration linked to the minerals they're looking for, whether those spots stand out statistically, what the licence and legal situation is, and what to do next on the ground.

What a survey is NOT (always say this upfront)

It does not “see” gold, copper or platinum directly. It does not measure how much ore there is (it is not a JORC or NI 43-101 resource report). It does not replace field sampling and drilling. It tells the client where it is worth looking, and where it isn't, before they spend money on fieldwork that costs 10 to 50 times more.

Why these prices are fair (what to tell clients)

  • A ground magnetic or drone survey of 30 km² costs about USD 23,000–98,000. Our Full Package is USD 12,000 and tells them where to put that fieldwork, or whether to skip it.
  • International junior geologists charge USD 500–600 a day. A T2 is about 10 analyst days for USD 4,000.
  • Payment terms: T1 100% in advance. T2 and above: 50% to start, 50% on delivery, which builds trust with new clients. Foreign clients: 100% in advance, in USD.

Report quality standard: lessons from the sample T4

Our sample T4 report was careful and honest, but it is not yet a polished client product. Every report must pass this checklist before delivery:

#CheckWhy
1Starts with a one-page plain-language summary: “What we found, what it means for you, what to do next”, in English, with an Urdu version on requestMost clients aren't geologists
2Report date = delivery date, and turnaround matches what we promisedThe sample was dated the same day the data arrived, which looks templated
3All tables fit the page; no overlapping text; section numbers continuousThe sample had broken tables
4Short glossary; internal codes (R9, G6, L12) removed or explainedJargon loses clients
5Clear verdict per block, and an honest explanation if there is “no target”Protects trust
6Next steps with indicative costs (for example, “ground magnetics: about PKR X”)Leads to the next sale
7Client name, licence number and coordinates double-checkedBasic professionalism
8Disclaimers: not a resource estimate, not investment adviceLegal protection

Golden rules for selling surveys

  • Set expectations at step 2: “We will tell you honestly what the satellites show, including if nothing stands out.”
  • Recommend the smallest package that answers their question. A happy T1 client becomes a T4 client.
  • Never promise minerals, grades or values.
  • Always end a meeting with a clear next step and a date.
  • Only take on as many surveys as the analysts can deliver on time. A late report damages us more than a lost sale.

Discovery questions for surveys

  1. Where is the land? Do you have a licence number or a Google Earth boundary?
  2. Which minerals are you interested in?
  3. What decision are you trying to make: buy, sell, invest, explore, or find a partner?
  4. What do you already know about the area (old reports, samples, workings)?
  5. When do you need an answer?
  6. Who else is involved in the decision?
  7. Have you budgeted for fieldwork? (This helps us show the value of screening first.)

Handling common objections

They say…We answer…
“Can satellites find gold or copper?”“Not directly, and anyone who says so is not being honest. Satellites map the rocks and alteration that these metals are found in. That tells you where to focus your field budget.”
“It's too expensive.”“A ground survey of the same area costs 10 to 50 times more. Our report tells you whether that spend is worth it. You can start with a Quick Screen at PKR 150,000.”
“Satellite data is free, why pay you?”“The pictures are free; the expertise isn't. We process several satellites, test the results statistically, add licence and legal checks, and explain it in plain language, in days.”
“The government has its own satellite now.”“Yes, SUPARCO's HS-1 is great for Pakistan. We focus on turning data into decisions for a specific licence, and we can work with any data source.”
“What if you find nothing?”“Then you've saved the cost of fieldwork on the wrong ground, and you get a clear recommendation on what to do next. We will always tell you the truth.”
“Is my information safe?”“We sign an NDA before we start, and we never share or publish your area.”
“I'll think about it.”“Of course. Let me send you a free snapshot of your area in 48 hours, so you can see what we do first.”
