# Pakistan-Specific Pricing Context for PakGeoLith (as of 3 Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-03. About 19 tool calls. Many primary sources (SBP rate sheets, FBR/PRA/SRB schedules, PPRA award notices, provincial mineral fee schedules) were not reachable or not indexed. Gaps are flagged in each section. Treat blog and aggregator figures as indicative only.

## 1. Exchange rate, inflation outlook, and whether to price foreign clients in USD

### Takeaway
The rupee has been very stable through 2026, at about Rs276.8–279 per USD. Inflation, however, has jumped back to double digits (10–11% in Aug–Sep 2026), and SBP started tightening again in late October 2026. PKR list prices will therefore lose real value quickly. Foreign clients should be priced in USD, and PKR prices should have annual indexation.

### Cited Findings
- Interbank close on 23 Oct 2026: about Rs276.82/USD, from about Rs276.85 the day before. The move was less than 0.1 rupee. — [PakTribune](https://paktribune.com/rupee-closes-at-276-82-per-dollar-after-another-marginal-gain/)
- Earlier 2026 readings: Rs278.90 (21 Apr 2026, ready market); SBP rate Rs278.7033 (8 May 2026); interbank Rs278.30 (16 Jun 2026), with open market buying at Rs279.05 and selling at Rs279.45. — [ARY Apr 21](https://arynews.tv/dollar-and-other-currency-rates-today-in-pakistan-april-21-2026), [ARY May 8](https://arynews.tv//dollar-currency-rates-in-pakistan-today-may-8-2026.amp), [ARY Jun 9](https://arynews.tv/currency-rates-today-in-pakistan-dollar-june-09-2026)
- CPI was 11.1% YoY in Aug 2026 and 10.3% in Sep 2026 (5.8% in Sep 2025). The Finance Ministry projected 9.5–10.5% for Oct 2026. — [pkrevenue.com, 29 Oct 2026](https://pkrevenue.com/pakistan-inflation-outlook-october-2026/), [The Pakistan Connect](https://thepakistanconnect.com/pakistan-inflation-eases-to-10-3-in-september/)
- SBP raised the policy rate by 50bp to 12%, effective 27 Oct 2026. — [pkrevenue.com](https://pkrevenue.com/pakistan-inflation-outlook-october-2026/)
- For contrast, Oct 2025 CPI was 6.2%. — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/11/03/pakistans-inflation-rises-to-6-2-in-october-higher-than-finance-ministry-forecast)

### Inferences
- Planning rate: about Rs277–280/USD. A 3% buffer for open-market spread and bank conversion gives a working rate of about Rs285/USD. Rupee stability depends on an IMF-backed managed float. Inflation near 10% against a flat currency means the real exchange rate is appreciating, which historically comes before step depreciations. PKR price lists should be reviewed every 6–12 months, with a CPI-linked escalation clause of about 10% a year in multi-year government and enterprise contracts.
- Price foreign, multinational and exporter clients in USD. That revenue can also qualify as IT/ITeS export for tax purposes (section 4) and gives forex retention benefits.

### Gaps
- No official SBP weighted-average rate for late Oct or early Nov 2026 was found. Check sbp.org.pk before publishing.
- The pkrevenue.com inflation figures are secondary. The official PBS October 2026 CPI release (normally out in the first days of November) was not found.

## 2. Purchasing power and regional pricing: global SaaS and Pakistani comparators

### Takeaway
Global consumer AI and SaaS products sell in Pakistan either at full USD price or with modest regional discounts. One example is ChatGPT Go at about Rs1,400/month, against Plus at about Rs5,700. Pakistani professionals' salaries are about 1/10 to 1/20 of Western levels, so domestic individual and SME tiers need deep PPP-style discounts (about 60–80% off USD list). Enterprise and foreign tiers can stay near international pricing.

### Cited Findings
- ChatGPT Plus is listed at about Rs5,700/month and ChatGPT Go, a cheaper tier launched for Pakistan, at about Rs1,400/month (Oct 2025). — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/10/10/chatgpt-go-brings-cheaper-gpt-5-access-to-pakistan/). A Pakistani reseller blog gives a different figure, $20 ≈ Rs8,400 including card charges and taxes. — [starsubspk blog](https://starsubspk.vercel.app/blog/best-ai-tools-subscriptions-pakistan-2025)
- Canva Pro: about Rs2,800/month billed annually, or $15/month (reseller blog, not verified against Canva). — [starsubspk blog](https://starsubspk.vercel.app/blog/best-ai-tools-subscriptions-pakistan-2025)
- Microsoft 365 Premium: $19.99/month in USD (same as ChatGPT Plus). No Pakistan rupee price was found. — [Engadget](https://engadget.com/ai/microsoft-365-premium-aims-to-deliver-more-ai-value-than-chatgpt-plus-160008060.html)
- Purchasing-power context: geologist pay of about Rs54k/month on average (section 3) is about $195/month. A Rs5,700 ChatGPT Plus subscription is about 10% of a mid-level geologist's monthly pay. — derived from [Payscale PK](https://www.payscale.com/research/PK/Job=Geologist/Salary)

### Inferences
- Suggested anchors: an individual or student tier at about Rs1,500–3,000/month (ChatGPT Go and Canva level); a professional or SME tier at about Rs5,000–15,000/month; and enterprise or API pricing in USD, at or near the international benchmark, sold annually.
- An annual prepaid discount (for example 2 months free) suits Pakistan because of inflation and card-friction issues (section 7).

### Gaps
- Current PKR prices for Adobe, LinkedIn Premium, Google Workspace and Zoom were not found. Check each vendor's checkout page from a PK IP.
- No published pricing was found for Pakistani B2B SaaS comparators (Bazaar, Abhi, Sastaticket B2B, local ERP/GIS vendors). Most use quote-based or commission models.

## 3. Pakistani professional fee levels (geologists, engineers, GIS, lawyers, CAs)

### Takeaway
Domestic geoscience labour is cheap: about Rs50k–220k/month, or roughly $180–800. Local lawyer hourly rates average about Rs2,200–2,700, or roughly $8–10. A PakGeoLith report priced in PKR competes against a few days of a local consultant's time, not against international consultancy rates.

### Cited Findings
- Geologists and geophysicists with more than 5 years of experience earn Rs93,812–222,821/month (48-hour week). — [WageIndicator](https://acc.wageindicator.org/en-pk/work-in-pakistan/jobs-and-pay/geologists-and-geophysicists)
- Geologist average pay is Rs650,000/year (2026), with a 25th–75th percentile range of about Rs98k–1m/year. — [Payscale](https://www.payscale.com/research/PK/Job=Geologist/Salary)
- 10% of geologists earn under Rs39k/month and 10% earn over Rs133k/month. — [Paylab PK](https://paylab.com/pk/salaryinfo/construction-real-estate/geologist)
- Dewan Cement geologist (2 years' experience) posting: Rs50k–80k/month. — [CareerOkay](https://www.careerokay.com/Job/job-listings-geogolist--104650)
- Overseas (Saudi) geologist posting via the Bureau of Emigration: $12,000, which shows how far international pay exceeds domestic. — [BEOE](https://beoe.gov.pk/foreign-jobs/4138447)
- Lawyer average hourly rate: about Rs2,211, about Rs2,684 in Islamabad and about Rs2,502 in Karachi (methodology unstated). — [Harvest calculator](https://www.getharvest.com/calculators/hourly-rate-calculator-in-pakistan)
- There is no official dataset for commercial lawyer fees in Pakistan. Karachi has the highest rates, and hourly, fixed and retainer models are all used. — [Global Law Experts](https://globallawexperts.com/commercial-lawyer-fees-pakistan/)
- Islamabad legal and tax firms on Clutch list $25–49/hour. — [Clutch PK legal](https://clutch.co/pk/consulting/legal)

### Inferences
- A rough fully loaded day rate for a mid-level local geologist, assuming 22 working days and a 2–3x consultancy markup on a Rs150k salary, is about Rs15k–20k/day (about $55–75). This is an estimate, not a sourced rate. A local licence-compliance report priced at Rs50k–150k is roughly 3–10 consultant-days, which is defensible. A USD 2,000+ survey report would need clear differentiation (satellite data, speed) for domestic buyers.
- Top-tier corporate law firms (Karachi and Lahore) likely bill well above the Harvest averages. No source was found to quantify this.

### Gaps
- No PEC consultancy fee guidelines or schedules were found online in this pass.
- No GIS consultancy day rates or chartered accountant due-diligence fee levels were found.
- No mining-engineer-specific salary data was found (Rozee and Glassdoor not indexed).

## 4. Taxes: provincial sales tax on IT services, withholding, IT export regime

### Takeaway
IT exports are taxed at a 0.25% final tax on proceeds, and the FY2026-27 budget (presented 12 Jun 2026) extended this regime to 30 Jun 2029. Exporters registered with PSEB or P@SHA can keep 50% of proceeds (or $5,000/month, whichever is higher) in a foreign-currency account. Domestic sales attract provincial sales tax on services:
- 16% standard in Punjab and ICT.
- 15% standard in Sindh and KP.
- IT and software may get reduced rates without input tax: about 5% in Punjab and historically 3% in Sindh (both unconfirmed for 2026-27).

### Cited Findings
- The 0.25% Final Tax Regime on IT and ITeS export proceeds was due to expire on 30 Jun 2026. Budget 2026-27 proposals (12 Jun 2026) extend it to 30 Jun 2029. — [CW Pakistan](https://cwpakistan.com/budget-2026-27-extends-it-sector-final-tax-regime-three-years-until-june-2029/), [Nukta](https://nukta.com/pakistan-budget-super-tax-cut-it-relief)
- Before the budget, P@SHA asked for the 0.25% regime to be kept for 10 years. — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/05/30/psha-wants-0-25-tax-regime-retained-for-it-exporters-genuine-freelancers/), [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2645446)
- Reports during the budget said the government planned a much heavier tax on IT companies ("300% higher"). The final outcome is unclear from the sources. — [ProPakistani 12 Jun 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/06/12/govt-to-impose-300-higher-tax-on-it-companies-in-budget/)
- The Budget 2026-27 abolished super tax for businesses earning up to Rs500m and cut it from 10% to 8% above that. — [Nukta](https://nukta.com/pakistan-budget-super-tax-cut-it-relief)
- The status of the Section 65F 0% exemption after Jun 2026 is unconfirmed. One blog says the default rate is 1% for unregistered exporters. — [beancount.io guide, Aug 2026](https://beancount.io/es/blog/2026/08/13/pakistan-it-freelancers-0-25-percent-pseb-2029-guide), [cssprep](https://cssprep.com.pk/freelancer-income-tax-pakistan/)
- SBP raised the IT exporter retention limit in ESFCAs from 35% to 50% (Oct 2023). It was still current in Jan 2026: $5,000/month or 50%, whichever is higher. Eligibility requires PSEB or P@SHA registration. — [SBP press release](https://www.sbp.org.pk/press/2023/Pr-23-Oct-2023-1.pdf), [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2023/10/23/sbp-increases-permissible-retention-limit-of-it-exporters-from-35-to-50), [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40415048)
- Punjab: the standard rate of 16% is unchanged. The Punjab Finance Act 2026 reportedly raised the reduced rate on many services from 5% to 8%. IT and software are listed at 5% without input tax, and one guide says the IT sector rate is the same as in FY2025-26. Consultancy blogs only. — [ConSerIC PST 2026-27](https://conseric.pk/updates-punjab-sales-tax-2026-27/), [ConSerIC rates](https://conseric.pk/punjab-sales-tax-on-services/), [Faubix](https://einvoicing.faubix.com/blog/pra-punjab-sales-tax-changes-2026)
- Sindh: the standard rate has been 15% since 1 Jul 2024. IT, software and call centres were cut to 3% without input credit in 2022, and EY (Oct 2023) describes IT at 13%, with 3% collected by agents on foreign remittances. — [EY tax alert](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/pakistan---sindh-issues-rules-for-collecting-sales-tax-on-it-and), [Taxation.pk Sindh FA 2026](https://taxationpk.com/srb-sales-tax-rates-2026-in-sindh-finance-act-2026/)
- Sindh targets Rs450bn in services sales tax for FY2026-27, with IT named as a focus sector. — [Profit 18 Jun 2026](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/06/18/sindh-targets-rs450-billion-in-sales-tax-on-services-for-fy2026-27)
- KP standard services rate: 15% (KP Sales Tax on Services Act 2022; aggregator checked Sep 2026). — [lookuptax](https://lookuptax.com/docs/country/pakistan)
- ICT standard rate: 16%. — [ConSerIC ICT](https://conseric.pk/ict-sales-tax-on-services/)
- A 5% digital services tax on foreign vendors with a significant digital presence was proposed. Its enactment is unconfirmed. — [Trustpoint Budget Brief 2026-27](https://www.trustpointconsultants.com/finance-bill-2026-27.html)

### Inferences
- Domestic PKR prices should be quoted "exclusive of applicable provincial sales tax", since the rate depends on where the service is rendered or the recipient is located (16%/15%/reduced IT rate). Government buyers will also deduct income tax withholding at source.
- Reports sold to foreign clients and paid in USD through banking channels should qualify as IT/ITeS export, giving the 0.25% final tax and the 50% FX retention. This requires PSEB registration, and the reports must be classed as IT-enabled services (data and analytics, not geological consultancy). A tax advisor needs to confirm this classification. Provincial sales tax on exported services is normally zero-rated or exempt, but this was not confirmed from a primary source.

### Gaps
- No primary-source 2026-27 schedules were found from PRA, SRB, KPRA, BRA or the FBR ICT schedule for IT services.
- Balochistan (BRA) rate: not found.
- Federal withholding rates on services in the Finance Act 2026 (s.153), and the PSEB registration fee: not found.
- No authoritative ruling was found on whether geological or satellite reports count as "IT-enabled services".

## 5. Government procurement pricing norms

### Takeaway
No contract values were found for Pakistani mining cadastre or GIS work, including the KP/Punjab cadastres, Landfolio and the GSP remote sensing tender. The only sourced norm is the PPRA rule: procurements above Rs3 million must be advertised on the PPRA website and in print. Punjab's DG Mines & Minerals runs a GIS project and tenders through PPRA and EPADS.

### Cited Findings
- Federal PPRA rules require procurements above Rs3 million to be advertised on the PPRA website and in print media. — [PPRA Rules](https://www.ppra.org.pk/rules.asp)
- The Punjab Directorate General of Mines & Minerals (GIS Project Director) tendered GIS hardware via the PPRA and EPADS portals. Value not stated. — [mnm.punjab.gov.pk tender](https://mnm.punjab.gov.pk/system/files/HardwareTender.pdf)

### Inferences
- Keeping government data licences and white-label portal deals under about Rs3m (about $10.8k) per contract, for example annual subscriptions, would likely avoid full open-tender advertising at federal level. Provincial PPRA thresholds differ and need checking. Larger portal builds should be expected to go through EPADS tenders with performance guarantees.
- Long payment cycles in the public sector are widely reported. Contracts should include milestone billing and a mobilisation advance. No source was found to quantify delays.

### Gaps
- No contract values were found for the KP or Punjab mining cadastres, Landfolio, the GSP remote sensing tender (2024) or PITB GIS projects. Check the PPRA, KPPRA and PPRA-Punjab award notices directly.
- Provincial procurement thresholds (Punjab and KP rules) and typical payment-delay data were not found.

## 6. Mine owners' willingness to pay (rents, fees, royalties, revenues)

### Takeaway
The legal frameworks were confirmed (Balochistan Mineral Rules 2002; KP Minerals Act 2017), but current rupee rent and fee schedules were not extracted. The available evidence suggests title-holding costs are low in absolute terms. One example is the KP late-rent penalty of Rs10,000/month. This supports modest PKR pricing for compliance reports aimed at small leaseholders.

### Cited Findings
- Balochistan Mineral Rules 2002 (2nd ed. 2008): annual rental is set per the Second Schedule, and application and demarcation fees per the First Schedule. Licence sizes and terms:
  - Reconnaissance licence: 100–10,000 km², up to 12 months.
  - Exploration licence: up to 1,000 km², up to 3 years.
  - Mining lease: up to 250 km², 10–30 years.
  - Also: a one-time 10% penalty for non-payment of royalty. — [Balochistan Minerals Rules PDF (MMD)](https://mmd.balochistan.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/balochistan-minerals-rules-2002.pdf), [nasirlawsite text](http://nasirlawsite.com/laws/mcrbaloc.htm)
- KP Mines and Minerals Act 2017: rent is "prescribed" per area. The penalty for late annual rent on a mining lease is Rs10,000 per month. — [KP Code](https://kpcode.kp.gov.pk/uploads/THE_KHYBER_PAKHTUNKHWA_MINES_AND_MINERALS_ACT_2017.pdf)
- Exploration licence terms (secondary source): about 3 years in Sindh and Punjab, 4 in Balochistan and 8 in KP. — [Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=1736f607-7c5b-49ab-999b-ff1f0f1c2466)
- Punjab (Pakistan) Minor Mineral Rules 2013 are held by the Punjab Mines & Minerals department. Fee schedule not extracted. — [Josh & Mak](https://joshandmakinternational.com/procedure-for-grant-of-mining-concessions-punjab/)
- Do not use the "Rs1,500/acre dead rent" and "Rs5,000 application fee" figures that come up in searches. They are from the Indian Punjab Minor Mineral Concession Rules 1964. — [revenue.punjab.gov.in (India)](https://revenue.punjab.gov.in/?q=node/755)

### Inferences
- Most leaseholders are small operators (marble, coal, chromite, construction minerals). Domestic compliance reports should likely be priced in the tens of thousands of PKR (about Rs25k–100k), not the hundreds of thousands. This is an inference, not a sourced figure.

### Gaps
- Current per-km² rents, application fees and royalty rates (KP 2017 rules, Punjab 2013/2002, Balochistan schedules, Sindh) were not found.
- Typical small-mine revenue data was not found.

## 7. Payment norms

### Takeaway
Pakistan has moved quickly to digital payments: 92% of retail transactions by volume are digital, and Raast handled 742 million transactions in Jan–Mar 2026. Credit cards are rare (about 4% of 61 million cards), so subscriptions should support debit cards, Raast and bank transfer or IBFT, and wallets. Annual prepayment by bank transfer is the natural pattern for businesses.

### Cited Findings
- Digital channels handled about 92% of retail transactions in Q2 FY26 (Oct–Dec 2025), up from 88% a year earlier. Q3 FY26 (Jan–Mar 2026) was also 92%, with 3.7bn retail transactions. — [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1983912/WhatsApp:/send), [Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2615321/electronic-payments-reach-37b-transactions)
- Raast processed 742m transactions worth Rs23.27tn in Jan–Mar 2026, up from 645.7m (Rs18.5tn) in Oct–Dec. Person-to-merchant volume was 55.9m, up from 36.3m. — [SBP PS Review Q3FY26](https://www.sbp.org.pk/psd/pdf/PS-Review-Q3FY26.pdf), [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/03/19/majority-of-digital-payments-now-done-via-mobile-banking-apps-in-pakistan-sbp/)
- 61.3m payment cards were in circulation (Q1 FY26): 90% debit, 4% credit. — [SBP Pr 30 Dec 2025](https://archive.sbp.org.pk/press/2025/Pr-30-Dec-2025.pdf), [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/12/30/retail-payments-hit-2-8-billion-transactions-worth-rs166-trillion-in-q1-fy26-sbp)
- Debit cards were 57.2m in Q3 FY26, up from about 25m six years earlier. — [Gallup Pakistan](https://galluppakistandigitalanalytics.com/2026/07/27/six-years-ago-roughly-25-million-debit-cards-were-in-circulation-across-pakistans-banks-today-q3-fy26-that-number-stands-at-57-2-million-more-than-double/)
- The SBP told banks to issue debit cards on ESFCA (IT exporter FX) accounts for online payments. — [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40415048)

### Inferences
- Use a local payment gateway with debit card and Raast or IBFT support for monthly PKR plans. For SME and enterprise clients, offer annual invoices payable by bank transfer, with advance payment as the norm. Government buyers pay in arrears after delivery or acceptance, so milestone billing is needed. Card-on-file recurring billing will have low uptake given how few credit cards exist.

### Gaps
- No data was found on B2B advance-payment acceptance, or on annual versus monthly billing preferences among Pakistani SaaS buyers.
