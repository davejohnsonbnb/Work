# International fee benchmarks for mineral, gemstone and commodity marketplaces, auctions and trust services (for re-pricing PakGeoLith's marketplace)

Research date: 3 November 2026. FX: PKR 278/USD (the brief's figure; I did not check a newer rate). PKR conversions are rounded and are my own arithmetic.
Scope note: I made about 20 tool calls. Many fee schedules are behind logins or don't publish prices (Gem Rock Auctions, Bonas, PGJDC, SGS/ALS USD lists, NADRA Verisys). The Gaps sections list these so the report writer doesn't fill them in.

## Gem and mineral auctions / online gem marketplaces: seller commissions, buyer's premiums, listing fees

### Takeaway
International gem and collectibles platforms almost never charge a flat per-lot listing fee. They take a percentage. Online gem auction platforms split the take roughly 12.5% from the seller plus 9% from the buyer (Catawiki), or about 15% from the seller (eBay jewelry). High-end auction houses now charge buyers up to 28% at the entry tier (Sotheby's 2026). A flat PKR 5,000/lot (about USD 18) is out of line with these models. The benchmarks support a success-based commission split between seller and buyer, with tender-house-style lower rates for bulk or rough parcels.

### Cited Findings
- **Sotheby's (from 13 Feb 2026, worldwide):** buyer's premium is 28% of hammer up to £1.5m/$2m, 22% from £1.5m–£6m ($2m–$8m), and 15% above £6m/$8m. The 2024 structure had 20% up to £5m/$6m. Lower-tier caps rose 60–105% depending on location. — [Antiques Trade Gazette](https://www.antiquestradegazette.com/news/2026/sotheby-s-raises-buyer-s-premium-to-28-at-the-lower-tier/); [The Value](https://en.thevalue.com/articles/sothebys-raises-buyers-premium-percentage-and-tiers-2026); [The Art Newspaper, 17 Feb 2026](https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2026/02/17/sothebys-adjusts-buyers-premiums-fee-structures-securitisation)
- **Christie's and Bonhams:** an Antiques Trade Gazette item dated September 2026 reports both changed their buyer's fees. Christie's 2026 tier percentages were not in the results. Christie's says its premium is tiered by price band. — [Antiques Trade Gazette topic page](https://www.antiquestradegazette.com/news/news-topics/buyers-and-sellers-premiums/); [Christie's help library](https://www.christies.com.cn/en/help/auction-help-library/understanding-auction-fees)
- **Catawiki (online curated auctions, including gemstones):** the buyer "Buyer Protection fee" is 9% of the final bid plus €3, with shipping excluded. The seller fee is "usually 12.5% of the purchase price and an additional fixed amount," excluding VAT. I found no separate gemstone rate. — [Catawiki buyer protection fee](https://auction.catawiki.com/en/help/fees/what-is-the-buyer-protection-fee); [Catawiki Terms of Use](https://www.catawiki.com/pages/p/terms-of-use)
- **eBay (US) jewelry category:** the final value fee is mostly reported as 15% on the sale total up to $5,000 and 9% above that, plus about $0.30–0.40 per order. Watches use a separate tiered schedule. These figures come from third-party 2026 guides, not eBay's own page, and one calculator gives 13%/7%. — [Taxomate 2026](https://taxomate.com/blog/ebay-seller-fees); [ecomcalctools: eBay jewelry fees 2026](https://ecomcalctools.com/blog/fees-ebay/ebay-jewelry-fees-2026/)
- **Gem Rock Auctions:** commission bills (which include the store/plan fee) are invoiced monthly for the prior month. Unpaid bills are auto-charged to the card on file after the 8th, and the account is suspended if payment fails. The site advertises auctions with "no buyers fees". The commission percentage itself could not be retrieved (403). — [Gem Rock Auctions help (zh)](https://www.gemrockauctions.com/zh/help/verified-sellers/commission-bills-and-selling-fees-explained); [Gem Rock help (en)](https://gemrockauctions.com/help/verified-sellers/commission-bills-and-selling-fees-explained)
- **Gemfields (Kagem, Zambia):** auctions are run by the miner itself. Royalties to the Zambian government are paid on the full auction sale prices. Gemfields has complained of an effective 21% turnover burden (export duty plus 6% mineral royalty). There have been 48 Kagem auctions since July 2009 with about $1.04bn total revenue, and a May 2024 higher-quality auction raised $35m. — [Mining Weekly, May 2024](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/gemfields-earns-35m-from-may-auction-of-higher-quality-emeralds-2024-05-31); [Miningmx](https://www.miningmx.com/uncategorized/33840-emerald-market-on-way-back-despite-indian-headwinds-gilbertson/)
- **Bonas (Antwerp tender house):** describes itself as the largest independent diamond and gemstone tender house. Its tenders are invitation-only with secure viewing rooms. In September 2025 it offered about 850,000 ct in about 900 lots worth about $90m, and a DRC artisanal (OrigemA) tender averaged $65.5/ct. No commission or fee schedule is published. — [Rapaport](https://rapaport.com/news/850000cts-of-rough-diamonds-to-feature-in-four-simultaneous-antwerp-tenders/); [AWDC OrigemA results 2026](https://www.awdc.be/sites/default/files/2026-06/ENG_PressRelease_OrigemAResults.pdf)
- **Tanzania government gem auctions/markets:** a Mirerani tanzanite auction collected TSh 1.84bn with 69 firms participating. Government mineral trading centres were set up in March 2019. — [The Citizen](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzanite-auction-collects-sh1-8bn-2617688); [ENACT/ISS 2019](https://issafrica.org/enactafrica/research/trend-reports/efforts-to-curb-tanzanite-smuggling-make-tanzania-shine)

### Inferences
- Benchmarks for a PakGeoLith gem e-auction: seller commission of about 10–15% (Catawiki 12.5%, eBay 15%), and optionally a buyer's premium of about 5–10% (Catawiki 9%). A buyer's premium of 20–28% is a luxury-auction-house level and too high for B2B rough or parcel trade.
- Rough and parcel tender houses (Bonas, and miner-run auctions like Gemfields) are the closer analogue for parcel e-auctions. They don't publish rates, which suggests negotiated low single-digit seller commissions. This is unverified.
- A flat PKR 5,000/lot (about USD 18) is close to eBay's insertion-fee logic only in being per lot. Benchmarks favour a small or zero listing fee plus a success fee.

### Gaps
- Exact Christie's, Bonhams and Heritage 2026 premium tiers, and seller commission schedules for all major houses (usually negotiated and unpublished).
- Gem Rock Auctions commission % and seller plan prices (page returned 403). Etsy fees were not researched.
- Bonas, I. Hennig, DMCC / Dubai Diamond Exchange tender commissions. I found no public schedule.
- Sri Lanka NGJA auctions, Myanmar Gems Emporium fees, and Thai gem auction fees. Nothing found within the call budget.

## Gem lab certification costs (per stone)

### Takeaway
Gübelin New York's published USD list shows that a coloured stone report starts at USD 280 without origin and USD 560 with origin for stones under 1 ct, rising to USD 1,320 / 2,640 for stones of 50 ct and over. GIA prices coloured stones in flat fees by carat band. Pakistani lab (PGJDC) fees are not published online. Origin reports from top labs therefore cost about PKR 78,000–156,000 per stone at the low end, so they only make sense for high-value lots. Local identification reports are needed for parcel lots.

### Cited Findings
- **Gübelin Gem Lab New York price list** (labelled "NY 2025", uploaded Jan 2026). Fees are USD per stone and include the report. The extracted carat bands are partly garbled; my reading of them is below.
  - Coloured stone report without origin: up to 0.99 ct $280; next bands $330, $440, $550, $610; 11–14.99 ct $660; 15–19.99 ct $770; 20–24.99 ct $940; 25–29.99 ct $1,050; 30–49.99 ct $1,210; 50 ct and over $1,320.
  - With origin: exactly double ($560 up to 0.99 ct through $2,640 for 50 ct and over).
  - Origin service is available for ruby, sapphire, emerald, alexandrite, Paraíba tourmaline, spinel and demantoid. If origin can't be determined, only the authenticity fee is charged.
  - Other products: Gem Passport $1,220; Gemmological Profile booklet $1,300. Diamond reports for colourless stones under 1 ct cost $410 (short report $300).
  - Source: [Gübelin GGL Preisliste NY 2025 (PDF)](https://gubelingemlab.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/GGL_Preisliste_NY_2025.pdf)
- **GIA coloured stones:** flat fees by carat band. For example, the ZAR schedule effective Jan 2025 starts at R1,300 for 0.01–19.99 ct, with a per-stone/per-gram column for large items. A Q4 2024 USD coloured stone schedule exists, but I could not extract it. GIA's natural coloured diamond schedule (Q2 2026 USD) lists a 0.30–0.49 ct band at $151 plus a rough submission fee. — [GIA ColoredStones FeeSchedule ZAR Q1 2025](https://gia.edu/doc/ColoredStones_FeeSchedule_Q1_2025_ZAR.pdf); [GIA ColoredStones USD Q4 2024](https://gia.edu/doc/ColoredStones_FeeSchedule_Q4_2024_USD.pdf); [GIA ColoredDiamond Q2 2026 USD](https://www.gia.edu/doc/ColoredDiamond_FeeSchedule_Q2_2026-USD.pdf)
- **SSEF, GRS, AGL:** only dealer claims were found (for example, SSEF "starting at several hundred dollars and scaling with stone value"), with no official price lists. Low reliability. — [Spectra Fine Jewelry blog](https://www.spectrafinejewelry.com/blogs/news/ssef-vs-gubelin-vs-gia-vs-agl-which-lab-report-matters-most)
- **PGJDC (Pakistan):** runs gem identification labs (five reported in 2013) in cities including Muzaffarabad and Sargodha, issuing reports and certificates. Fees are not published. KP announced in 2021 that it would set up an international-standard gem testing lab in Peshawar. — [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/105417); [PGJDC](https://pgjdc.org/company-initiatives.php?catId=24); [ProPakistani 2021](https://propakistani.pk/2021/01/07/kp-to-establish-standard-lab-for-testing-and-identifying-local-gemstones/amp/)

### Inferences
- At $560 for a with-origin Gübelin report on a sub-1 ct stone (about PKR 155,700), international origin reports are only economical for single stones worth several thousand USD or more. PakGeoLith could offer tiered verification: local lab identification for parcels, and an international lab as a pass-through option for premium lots.
- Gübelin's pricing doubles for origin. That gives a useful ratio for any PakGeoLith "provenance-verified" upsell.

### Gaps
- PGJDC and Peshawar lab per-stone fees (contact info@pgjdc.org). Exact current GIA USD coloured stone bands. SSEF, GRS and AGL official schedules.

## Bulk mineral marketplaces / e-auctions and government mineral marketing

### Takeaway
Government e-auction operators charge very low success fees on ore and mineral sales: MSTC charges 0.2% of sale value for ores and minerals, against 1.6% for general e-auction disposal. B2B directories charge annual subscriptions instead: Alibaba Gold Supplier about USD 4,700/year, and IndiaMART's catalogue plan INR 35,000/year. Producer-country regimes add roughly 1% inspection or clearing fees on top of royalties.

### Cited Findings
- **MSTC (India):**
  - Odisha's finance circular sets MSTC e-auction charges at 1.60% of the value of materials disposed of, but 0.2% for e-sale of ores and minerals. — [Odisha Finance circular](https://finance.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/finance_circulars/20914.pdf)
  - Goa's 31st mineral e-auction procedure lists MSTC service charges at 0.2% of sale value. — [Goa DMG 31st E-Auction Procedure](https://dmg.goa.gov.in/PDFs/E-Auction/31st-E-Auction-Procedure.pdf)
  - MSTC says most of its agreements are percentage-based, with a few flat-fee ones. A West Bengal approval for scrap allowed up to 3%. — [MSTC e-commerce services](https://mstcindia.co.in/content/ecommerce_services.aspx); [wbxpress](https://wbxpress.com/subject/mstc/)
- **Alibaba.com:** a 2026 Alibaba seller blog lists Gold Supplier at $4,700/year. A third-party guide cites Verified Supplier at about $12,500/year (UK). Another summary cites Verified from CNY 29,800/year (2025). Prices vary by region. — [Alibaba seller blog 2026](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/food-beverage/gold-supplier-membership-guide-alibaba-b2b); [Jing Sourcing](https://jingsourcing.com/b-alibaba-gold-supplier/)
- **IndiaMART Mini Dynamic Catalog:** INR 35,000 for 1 year, INR 60,000 for 2 years, INR 72,000 for 3 years (excluding taxes). TrustSEAL is bundled into the MDC/Maximiser plans. Star Supplier is a premium add-on with no published price. A third-party site quotes higher MDC figures (INR 90,000 for 2 years; about INR 18/lead). — [IndiaMART corporate: MDC](https://corporate.indiamart.com/indiamart-paid-service-mdc/); [IndiaMART help](https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/mdc); [Refrens](https://www.refrens.com/grow/detailed-overview-and-analysis-of-all-indiamart-packages/)
- **Tanzania:**
  - Reported charges include a 6% royalty on gemstones and a 1% inspection/clearance fee on the gross value of exported minerals, plus 18% VAT and 5% withholding tax (2019 tanzanite report). — [ENACT/ISS](https://issafrica.org/enactafrica/research/trend-reports/efforts-to-curb-tanzanite-smuggling-make-tanzania-shine); [TanzaniaInvest: 1% clearing fee](https://tanzaniainvest.com/mining/1-percenyt-clearing-fee-mineral-export)
  - For gold, the Bank of Tanzania purchase programme offers a 4% royalty instead of 6% and no inspection fee. — [TanzaniaInvest](https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/?p=27665)
  - The USGS gives a conflicting 7% figure. — [USGS 2019 Minerals Yearbook](https://pubs.usgs.gov/myb/vol3/2019/myb3-2019-tanzania.pdf)
- **Zambia:** Gemfields cites an effective 21% turnover burden, combining export duty with a 6% mineral royalty. — [Miningmx](https://www.miningmx.com/uncategorized/33840-emerald-market-on-way-back-despite-indian-headwinds-gilbertson/)

### Inferences
- For bulk minerals such as chromite and coal, a commission of 0.2–1% of sale value is the defensible benchmark (MSTC 0.2% for ores). It would earn far more than PKR 5,000 on a USD 50,000 chromite lot (0.5% = USD 250, about PKR 69,500) and less on small lots, so a minimum fee is sensible.
- An annual seller membership could run from about INR 35,000 (about USD 400, IndiaMART) to USD 4,700 (Alibaba). For PakGeoLith, a PKR-denominated tier of roughly PKR 50,000–300,000/year with bundled verification falls within the international range. This range is my inference.

### Gaps
- Coal India e-auction fees, Metal.com, Fastmarkets, Metalbook, ScrapMonster, Tradeindia, LME/ICE fees, Zambia MMCZ commission, Zimbabwe MMCZ commission (reported elsewhere as about 0.875%, but not verified here). None were researched within the call budget, or the searches returned nothing.

## Assay and inspection costs

### Takeaway
ALS publishes a chromite/manganese ore fusion-XRF method (ME-XRF26s) but no public USD price in the results. A university lab's fusion-XRF major-element analysis is about R225 (about USD 12–18) per sample. Commercial lab assays are a per-sample pass-through cost in the tens of USD. I could not confirm pre-shipment inspection fees.

### Cited Findings
- **ALS:** ME-XRF26s (chromite and manganese ore; Al2O3, Cr2O3, Fe2O3, MgO, SiO2 and others, plus LOI; fusion XRF on a 0.33 g sample). The ALS 2024 fee schedule is published in EUR, but the price line could not be extracted. — [ALS industrial minerals](https://alsglobal.com/da/geochemistry/energy-minerals-analysis/industrial-minerals); [ALS Geochemistry Fee Schedule EUR 2024](https://www.alsglobal.com/-/media/ALSGlobal/Resources-Grid/Fee-Schedule-Geochemistry/2024/ALS-Geochemistry-Fee-Schedule-EUR-2024.pdf)
- **Stellenbosch University CAF:** "XRF: Majors on fusion, incl fusion disk & LOI" costs about R225 (internal/basic tier). A USD figure of about $17.8 appears, but the tier mapping is unclear. — [Stellenbosch ICP-MS & XRF 2024 price list](https://www.sun.ac.za/english/research-innovation/caf/Documents/ICP-MS%20%26%20XRF%202024%20Price%20List_01.pdf)
- **Geological Survey of Tanzania:** publishes a laboratory price list (in Swahili). Figures were not extracted. — [GST price list](https://www.gst.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1747303122-Price%20List%20_Laboratory%20services.doc.pdf)

### Inferences
- Assay should be offered as a pass-through charge with a PakGeoLith coordination margin rather than folded into the listing fee.

### Gaps
- SGS, Bureau Veritas and Intertek price lists, pre-shipment inspection fees (usually quoted per job or as a % of FOB), and PCSIR and GSP lab rates. None were found within the budget.

## Escrow, LC and payment fees

### Takeaway
Escrow.com's standard fee is 2.6% (minimum $50) up to $5,000, falling to 1.9% at $50k–200k and 1.0% at $1m–3m. In Pakistan, HBL import LC commission is 0.30–0.40% for the first quarter, with a PKR 2,500 minimum plus PKR 2,000 SWIFT, for July–December 2026. Card PSP Safepay charges 2.9% + PKR 30 domestic. Raast P2M is free for payers, and SBP has asked providers to waive merchant fees.

### Cited Findings
- **Escrow.com (USD, merchandise):**

  | Transaction amount | Standard fee | Concierge fee |
  |---|---|---|
  | $0–5,000 | 2.6% (min $50) | 5.2% (min $100) |
  | $5,000.01–50,000 | 2.4% (min $130) | 4.8% |
  | $50,000.01–200,000 | 1.9% (min $1,200) | 3.8% |
  | $200,000.01–500,000 | 1.5% | 3.0% |
  | $500,000.01–1m | 1.2% | 2.4% |
  | $1m–3m | 1.0% | 2.0% |
  | $3m–5m | 0.95% | 1.9% |
  | $5m–10m | 0.9% | 1.8% |
  | $10m+ | 0.7% | 1.4% |

  Other charges: card/PayPal payment adds 3.05% (only up to $5k); international inbound wire adds $25; disbursement wires are $10 domestic and $20 international. — [Escrow.com fee calculator](https://www.escrow.com/fee-calculator)
- **HBL Schedule of Bank Charges, 1 Jul–31 Dec 2026** (excluding FED/ST):
  - Import LC opening commission, by annual volume:

    | Annual LC volume | 1st quarter | Each later quarter |
    |---|---|---|
    | Up to Rs 25M | 0.40% | 0.25% |
    | Rs 25–50M | 0.35% | 0.20% |
    | Rs 50–100M | 0.30% | 0.20% |
    | Above Rs 100M | Negotiable | Negotiable |

    The minimum is Rs 2,500 per LC, plus Rs 2,000 SWIFT and Rs 4,000 foreign courier.
  - Inland LC opening: 0.50% for the first quarter and 0.35% for later quarters, minimum Rs 2,500, plus Rs 500 SWIFT.
  - LC advising: Rs 2,000 flat for HBL customers. Inland LC advising/amendment: Rs 1,500. Confirmation is priced case by case.
  - Guarantees: 0.45–0.60% per quarter (minimum Rs 1,500). The Islamic schedule lists LC opening at Rs 2,500 per LC.
  - Source: [HBL SOBC Jul–Dec 2026 (PDF)](https://www.hbl.com/assets/documents/Schedule_of_Bank_Charges_for_July_to_December_2026_%E2%80%93_English.pdf); [HBL Islamic SOBC Jul–Dec 2026](https://www.hbl.com/assets/documents/Islamic_Schedule_of_Bank_Charges_for_July_to_December_2026_%E2%80%93_English.pdf)
- **Safepay:** 2.9% + Rs 30 for domestic and 3.2% + Rs 30 for international transactions (excluding tax). An older help page cites a 2.5% example. — [Safepay fee breakdown](https://safepay.helpscoutdocs.com/article/131-safepay-fee-breakdown); [Safepay pricing](https://safepay.helpscoutdocs.com/article/120-how-much-does-safepay-charge)
- **Raast P2M:** SBP's December 2023 circular bars charging payers. Merchant service providers may charge merchants but are encouraged to waive initially. A government subsidy of Rs 3.5bn/year for 3 years has been reported, with per-transaction support of about the lower of 0.5% or PKR 200 (reports vary). — [SBP PSP&OD Circular 4 of 2023](https://www.sbp.org.pk/psd/2023/C4.htm); [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40380075)

### Inferences
- PakGeoLith-run escrow could be priced at about 1–2.5% with a PKR minimum, mirroring Escrow.com tiers. Settlement via Raast keeps payment costs near zero, whereas cards cost about 3% (Safepay).
- For cross-border buyers, LC costs (0.3–0.4% plus about PKR 4,500 fixed at HBL on the import side; export-side costs differ) are cheaper than Escrow.com on large lots but heavier in paperwork. These are useful comparators for the escrow pricing.

### Gaps
- MCB and Meezan schedules and export-side LC fees were not extracted. Raast merchant fee schedules of individual banks or PSPs were not found.

## Seller verification / KYC fees

### Takeaway
Verification is priced either as part of a subscription (Alibaba Verified about USD 12,500/year, IndiaMART TrustSEAL bundled) or as a per-check cost passed on (NADRA CNIC checks are historically about PKR 10–99 per verification).

### Cited Findings
- **NADRA:** agreed in 2018 to cut its verification fee from Rs 45 to Rs 10 per verification. Consumer SMS verification costs Rs 10. Easypaisa charged Rs 99 per biometric inquiry in 2024, citing a NADRA requirement. Current Verisys commercial rates are contract-based and unpublished. — [Express Tribune 2018](https://tribune.com.pk/story/1615536/nadra-agrees-reduce-cost-verification); [ProPakistani 2024](https://propakistani.pk/2024/09/18/easypaisa-is-charging-people-for-failing-biometric-verification/); [Zameen](https://www.zameen.com/blog/cnic-nadra-verification.html)
- **Alibaba Verified Supplier** (on-site third-party audit): about $12,500/year for UK suppliers per a third-party guide. Gold Supplier costs $4,700/year. — [Jing Sourcing](https://jingsourcing.com/b-alibaba-gold-supplier/); [Alibaba seller blog](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/food-beverage/gold-supplier-membership-guide-alibaba-b2b)
- **IndiaMART TrustSEAL:** included within MDC/Maximiser plans (MDC from INR 35,000/year). — [IndiaMART help](https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/mdc)

### Inferences
- The data cost of KYC per seller is trivial (PKR 10–100 for the NADRA check). A PakGeoLith "verified licensed seller" fee mainly needs to cover manual licence and mine-lease checks and any site visit. A one-time onboarding fee plus an annual renewal fee is consistent with these benchmarks.

### Gaps
- No current NADRA Verisys commercial rate card. No pricing found for third-party KYC vendors in Pakistan.

## Sponsored / featured listings and lead generation on B2B marketplaces

### Takeaway
Hard pricing is scarce. IndiaMART sells premium visibility as add-ons ("Star Supplier", with no public price), and one source puts IndiaMART's effective cost at about INR 18 per lead. eBay's promoted-listing model is an ad-rate percentage, but I did not verify the rates.

### Cited Findings
- **IndiaMART:**
  - Star Supplier is a premium add-on to MDC/Maximiser, with no price published. — [IndiaMART help](https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/mdc)
  - A third-party breakdown quotes about Rs 18/lead on a 2-year MDC. — [Refrens](https://www.refrens.com/grow/detailed-overview-and-analysis-of-all-indiamart-packages/)
  - An invoice on Scribd shows "IM STAR 10" category visibility at INR 75,000 for 1 year. This is a single, unverified document. — [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/815177907/PI)

### Inferences
- PakGeoLith could sell category-level featured placement as an annual or monthly flat fee, or price leads per RFQ. The IndiaMART reference points are INR 75,000/year for category visibility and about INR 18/lead.

### Gaps
- Alibaba keyword-advertising (P4P) CPC rates, eBay Promoted Listings rates and Tradeindia featured pricing were not verified within the budget.
