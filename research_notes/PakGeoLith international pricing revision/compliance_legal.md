# Compliance, Legal and Regulatory Intelligence Pricing Benchmarks (for PakGeoLith re-pricing)

Research date: 3 Nov 2026. FX used for conversions: PKR 278/USD (as instructed). Rough FX for other currencies (my own approximations, not sourced; check before publishing): GBP 1 = USD 1.30, AUD 1 = USD 0.65, CAD 1 = USD 0.72. Note: about 16 searches/fetches. Several primary fee schedules could not be fetched (WA 2025-26 PDF returned 404). Where a figure comes from an aggregator or blog, that is noted.

## 1. Mining tenement / title search and due-diligence report prices (international)

### Takeaway
Government registry searches are close to free (about USD 0-10 a search in WA and BC). The value, and the price, sits in professional interpretation. Law-firm title opinions and mining legal DD run from about USD 5k to well over USD 50k, and firms quote them by scope (claim count, chain complexity). None of the firms checked publish prices. A raw title/status lookup is therefore a commodity priced in single-digit USD. An interpreted compliance and title report competes with legal work priced in the thousands of USD.

### Cited Findings
- **Western Australia (DMIRS/DEMIRS):** the 2025-26 Minerals Fees & Charges schedule lists "search for mining tenement register" at AUD 10.60 (about USD 7). The search snippet surfaced this figure, but the PDF itself returned 404 when fetched — [WA minerals fees & charges 2025-26](https://www.wa.gov.au/system/files/2025-07/minerals_fees_charges2025-26.pdf). The 2017 schedule listed the same search at AUD 9.10 — [Scribd copy of 2017 schedule](https://www.scribd.com/document/599265223/Minerals-Feesandcharges-2017).
- **WA Mineral Titles Online:** there is no charge for a Mining Tenement Summary Report. The paid "Official" tenement data extract reflects the register as at the time of ordering. A free "Scheduled" extract omits some dealing types — [DMIRS MTO help 676](https://dmp.wa.gov.au/mtol_help/676.aspx); [DMIRS MTO help 505](https://dmp.wa.gov.au/mtol_help/505.aspx).
- **Queensland:** the Mineral Resources Regulation 2025 has been in force since 1 Sep 2025. Fees are expressed in fee units (value set under the Acts Interpretation (Fee Unit) Regulation 2022). I did not find a dollar amount for a tenure search — [QLD Mineral Resources Regulation 2025](https://www.legislation.qld.gov.au/view/pdf/inforce/2025-09-01/sl-2025-0105); [Business Queensland fees page](https://www.business.qld.gov.au/industries/mining-energy-water/resources/minerals-coal/authorities-permits/payments/fees-mining-resource).
- **Northern Territory:** publishes a quarterly "Schedule of mineral titles fees and rent", including a 01/10/2026–30/06/2027 edition (contents not reviewed) — [NT schedule 2026-27](https://nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0003/1623774/schedule-of-mineral-titles-fees-and-rent-01102026-to-30062027.pdf).
- **British Columbia (MTO), fee page updated 25 Mar 2025:** Free Miner Certificate CAD 25 for individuals, CAD 500 for corporations. Mineral claim registration CAD 1.75/ha (placer CAD 5/ha). Work requirement CAD 5–20/ha/yr, or cash in lieu of CAD 10–40/ha/yr. Document/notice registration CAD 10 per title. Complaint CAD 200 per claim. No search fee was mentioned (MTO viewing is generally open) — [BC Mineral and placer title fees](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/mineral-exploration-mining/mineral-titles/mineral-placer-titles/fees-mineral-placer-titles).
- **Zambia (Landfolio/FlexiCadastre):** portals were reactivated in June 2025 after a reported outage. Clients can access all cadastre services through the map portal and online transactional portal. I found no published search/extract fee — [Creamer Media, 12 Jun 2025](https://newsletter.en.creamermedia.com/article/zambias-south-africa-based-cadastre-system-developer-re-engaged-ministry-confirms-2025-06-12); [Creamer Media, 5 Jun 2025](https://newsletter.en.creamermedia.com/article/zambias-mining-licence-cadastre-system-is-reportedly-not-functioning-2025-06-05).
- **Law-firm mining title opinions:** I found no published fee schedules. One dated (2012) oil-and-gas forum estimate puts a title opinion at USD 5,000–50,000, "much closer to the lower end" typically. This is a weak source — [GoHaynesvilleShale forum](https://gohaynesvilleshale.com/xn/detail/2117179:Comment:2588634). Practice literature says fees scale with title-chain complexity, and legacy claims and leases raise the cost — [Mondaq: title searches, undersurface rights](https://www.mondaq.com/canada/land-law-agriculture/197312/understanding-title-searches-undersurface-rights); [FNREL Mineral Title Examination & DD 2024](https://www.fnrel.org/publications/bookstore/mineral-title-examination-review-and-due-diligence-for-oil-and-gas-and-mining-projects-2024).
- **Firms offering mineral title/DD services without listing prices:** Steptoe & Johnson ([service page](https://www.steptoe-johnson.com/service/mineral-title-due-diligence-services/)) and Bowles/BW Energy Law ([page](https://www.bwenergylaw.com/mineral-title-examination-due-diligence/)). A Harare firm (Kantor & Immerman) did title, ownership, mining-rights and compliance DD on Tinker Mining assets — [Itinerant.ie](https://www.itinerant.ie/news/article.html?id=6).

### Inferences
- A per-licence registry lookup is worth roughly USD 0–10 internationally. PakGeoLith should not price the "lookup" itself. It should price interpretation, cross-referencing to rules, and risk flags.
- An interpreted per-licence title and compliance memo sits between registry fees (about USD 10) and law-firm title opinions (about USD 5k+). For Pakistan, roughly USD 150–700 (PKR 40k–200k) per licence looks defensible as a "pre-legal screening" product, provided it carries a disclaimer that it is not a legal opinion. This is an inference, not a sourced benchmark.

### Gaps
- No published prices for ASX "Independent Solicitor's Reports on Tenements" (Hetherington, Steinepreis Paganin, etc.) or for Tenement Administration Services. A targeted search found nothing.
- No Chile (SERNAGEOMIN / Conservador de Minas) search fees found.
- No Tanzania or Mozambique Landfolio public search fees found.
- No published M&A legal-DD cost benchmarks specific to mining.

## 2. Pakistan: cadastre and title search fees, mining-concession fees, law-firm and verification pricing

### Takeaway
Pakistani official fees for land-record copies are low, at PKR 900 standard and PKR 4,000 express per fard in Punjab for FY2026-27. Mining-concession dealings carry six-figure PKR charges; the KP assignment (transfer) fee was reportedly raised to PKR 500,000. I found no published mining-cadastre search fee or law-firm fee schedule. The old PKR 30k per-licence report was therefore priced well below what a title transfer costs a client (PKR 500k), and well below what a law firm charges on a percentage basis.

### Cited Findings
- **Punjab Land Records Authority, FY2026-27:** a copy of fard, mutation or girdawari costs PKR 900 standard or PKR 4,000 express (express via Mobile Arazi Record Centers and Universal Access) — [ProPakistani, 8 Jul 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/07/08/punjab-increases-fees-for-land-record-services/); [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1242050); [Profit, 14 Jul 2026](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/07/14/punjab-raises-property-transaction-and-land-record-service-fees-for-fy2026-27). The previous FY2023-24 rate was PKR 600 per fard transaction, or USD 30 for overseas fard via consulates — [PLRA notification FY2023-24](https://punjab-zameen.gov.pk/assets/documents/notifications/PLRAServiceChargesFY2023-24.pdf). Tribune reported on 10 Aug 2026 that land record services had become costlier — [Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2618085/land-record-services-get-costlier).
- **KP mining title assignment (transfer):** the Hazara Mines & Mineral Association said the KP Mines & Minerals Department raised the assignment fee from PKR 132,000 to PKR 500,000. The date and current status are unconfirmed (search snippet only) — [search result set including KP sources](https://www.pakp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/The-KP-Mines-and-Minerals-Second-Amendment-Act2024.pdf). Treat this as unverified.
- **KP legal framework:** the KP Minerals Titles (large and small scale mining) Governance Rules 2017, the Auction Rules 2017 and the Minor Minerals Permit Rules 2017 continue in force. The 2024 Second Amendment Act revised royalty rates (Schedule IV-A) from 1 Jul 2024 — [KP Mines & Minerals Second Amendment Act 2024](https://www.pakp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/The-KP-Mines-and-Minerals-Second-Amendment-Act2024.pdf); [KP Minerals Governance Act 2017](https://www.pakp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/The-Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa-Minerals-Governance-Act-2017-Act-No.-XXXVI-2017.pdf). KP applications are filed online and fees are paid by treasury challan — [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1277109).
- **Punjab Mining Concession Rules 2002:** amended repeatedly (2016–2025, including 13 Jan 2025). I did not find the fee schedule — [Punjab Mines & Minerals Dept notifications](https://mnm.punjab.gov.pk/node/71). In Jan 2026 the LHC nullified the markup sub-rules on mining dues — [Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2555408/lhc-nullifies-mining-dues-markup-sub-rules).
- **Pakistani law-firm property DD fees:** no published schedules. One blog aimed at overseas buyers estimates law-firm fees at about 1–3% of property value (marketing source, weak) — [Sohaib N Sultan blog, Jan 2026](https://sohaibnsultan.blogspot.com/2026/01/the-complete-guide-for-overseas.html). Typical DD scope is title, revenue record (fard), ownership chain, encumbrances and seller authority — [Global Law Company](https://global-law-company.pages.dev/practice-areas/due-diligence).
- **SECP:** has issued digital certified true copies of statutory returns and mortgage registers via eServices since 2021. In 2023 an SRO raised document-filing fees under the 7th Schedule of the Companies Act 2017. I did not find the current certified-copy or search fee — [ProPakistani 2021](https://propakistani.pk/2021/04/13/secp-launches-digital-certified-true-copies/); [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40239071).
- **NADRA Verisys:** the institutional rate was cut from PKR 45 to PKR 10 per verification in 2018 (dated) — [Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/1615536/nadra-agrees-reduce-cost-verification). Consumer SMS verification costs PKR 10 including tax — [Zameen blog](https://www.zameen.com/blog/cnic-nadra-verification.html). In Feb 2026 NADRA launched "Nishan Pakistan", which includes Verisys services; pricing was not reported — [ProPakistani Feb 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/02/10/nadra-introduces-nishan-pakistan-to-strengthen-secure-identity-verification/amp/).

### Inferences
- Pakistani official data inputs are cheap: PKR 900–4,000 for a fard, about PKR 10 for a CNIC check. Clients therefore pay mainly for assembly, interpretation and turnaround. A per-licence report needs to justify its price relative to the transaction it supports. Against a PKR 500k assignment fee and percentage-based legal fees, a per-licence screening at PKR 50k–150k (USD 180–540) would be under 1/3 of the transfer fee alone. This is an inference.
- An "express" tier is normal in Pakistani government services (PLRA charges 4.4x for express). That supports tiered turnaround pricing.

### Gaps
- No KP or Punjab mining-cadastre search/extract fee, and no current PMCR 2002 application, renewal or transfer fee table (gazette PDFs not reached).
- No Balochistan or Sindh fee data.
- No published Pakistani law-firm hourly rates or mining DD quotes.
- No PEC consultant rate schedules found (not searched in depth).
- No Zameen "verification service" price found.

## 3. Regulatory and legal intelligence product pricing

### Takeaway
Pakistani legal databases cost about PKR 36k/year (about USD 130). Global legal research tools run about USD 80–550 per user per month. Regulatory-change SaaS such as Regology is enterprise-priced, reportedly about USD 1,700 per user per month. A 40-instrument / 785-rule Pakistani mining register can reasonably sit above PakistanLawSite (general case law) but far below global SaaS. That points to about PKR 60k–250k a year per organisation (inference).

### Cited Findings
- **PakistanLawSite:** PKR 36,000 per year plus PKR 2,000 one-time registration; quarterly PKR 9,000; international USD 700 per year. One concurrent user per subscription — [pakistanlawsite.com](https://pakistanlawsite.com/). A third party reports PKR 38,000 for the first year and PKR 36,000 thereafter — [LawsHun](https://lawshun.com/article/how-to-find-case-law-in-pakistan).
- **Practical Law (Thomson Reuters Australia promo):** AUD 2,189 per year for one user (discounted). A Westlaw + Practical Law small-firm bundle under AUD 2,000 per year — [TR AU flash sale](https://www.thomsonreuters.com.au/en-au/c/wlpl-flashsale.html).
- **Westlaw:** third-party estimates range from USD 132.80 per month (single-state Classic, Dec 2024) to USD 225–500 per user per month. Westlaw Edge with AI starts at USD 155.35 per month on a 3-year term for small firms — [gc.ai](https://gc.ai/blog/westlaw-pricing); [Vaquill 2026](https://www.vaquill.ai/blog/legal-research-costs-2026-what-firms-pay); [aitoolsatlas](https://aitoolsatlas.ai/tools/westlaw/pricing). These are aggregator estimates.
- **Lexis+:** about USD 80–135 per user per month for core research, and about USD 250–550 per user per month with Lexis+ AI (aggregator estimates) — [aitoolsatlas Lexis+ AI](https://aitoolsatlas.ai/tools/lexis-plus-ai/pricing); [Vaquill](https://www.vaquill.ai/blog/legal-research-costs-what-firms-pay). Annual escalators run about 5–8%.
- **Regology:** a free "Reggi" tier. Professional is about USD 1,700 per user per month on a 3-year contract (about USD 20.4k per user per year). Enterprise is custom. Aggregators conflict on whether the price is per user — [SoftwareFinder](https://softwarefinder.com/governance-risk-compliance-software/regology); [ToolRadar](https://toolradar.com/tools/regology/pricing); [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/regology); [Regology pricing page](https://regology.com/pricing).
- **Compliance.ai:** no public price. Reportedly priced by number of jurisdictions monitored (weak source) — [Monetizely](https://www.getmonetizely.com/articles/how-can-financial-services-saas-companies-price-their-regulatory-compliance-solutions).

### Inferences
- Positioning: PakistanLawSite (PKR 36k per year for a general-purpose database) sets a local floor. A specialised, curated mining register with change alerts could sit at about 2–6x that: PKR 75k–200k per year per organisation (USD 270–720), with a foreign-investor tier in USD (for example USD 1,500–3,000 per year). PakistanLawSite's international price is about 5.4x its domestic price (USD 700 vs about USD 130). This is inference from the benchmarks.

### Gaps
- No pricing found for Mining Journal / Global Legal Insights mining guides, Lexology Getting the Deal Through, or Chambers mining guides. Most of these are free to read with registration; I did not verify this.
- No PLD (Pakistan Legal Decisions) or LEAP Pakistan prices retrieved. A LEAP purchase page exists — [leappakistan.com/purchase](https://leappakistan.com/purchase/) — but I did not fetch it.

## 4. ESG/compliance screening and KYC/seller verification costs

### Takeaway
Single-company ESG risk reports are reportedly about USD 150 (RepRisk, unverified). Automated KYC ID checks cost about USD 1–3 per check globally. Pakistani NADRA checks cost about PKR 10 (dated). A KYC/seller check in a PakGeoLith bundle is a low-cost component (under USD 5 of input cost). Price it as an add-on, not as a core product.

### Cited Findings
- **RepRisk Company Report:** USD 150 per report on a third-party listing (undated, partner-controlled content) — [Energy-XPRT](https://www.energy-xprt.com/books/reprisk-esg-business-intelligence-reports-46672). The report covers 2-year and 10-year risk metrics and incident details — [SoftwareOne listing](https://platform.softwareone.com/product/reprisk-reports/PCP-2469-0228).
- **Sumsub:** USD 1.35 per verification (Basic, USD 149 per month minimum) and USD 1.85 (Compliance, USD 299 per month minimum) — [CostBench Sumsub](https://costbench.com/software/kyc-aml/sumsub/); [xpay.sh](https://www.xpay.sh/saas-pricing/sumsub).
- **Veriff:** reportedly USD 0.80 / 1.39 / 1.89 per verification with USD 49 / 99 / 209 monthly minimums. Sources conflict — [CostBench Veriff](https://costbench.com/software/kyc-aml/veriff/).
- **Onfido (Entrust):** no public price. Third parties estimate about USD 2–3 per check — [tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/igt-smoke-onfido-vs-sumsub-resume-2026/).
- **NADRA Verisys:** PKR 10 per check for institutions as of 2018 — see section 2.

### Gaps
- No Sustainalytics or Kharon pricing found. Both are enterprise subscriptions with no public price.
- No Pakistani commercial KYC/KYB vendor price list found.

## 5. Location intelligence "site brief" / geological desktop report prices (for "5 km point-intelligence briefs" and point-geology tests)

### Takeaway
UK automated property geology, mining and environmental desktop reports are a mature, standardised market. Prices run from about GBP 39 (Geo Insight) to about GBP 75 (metalliferous/stone mining search), about GBP 111 (Enviro Insight) and about GBP 199 (full Insight bundle), all ex-VAT. That is roughly USD 50–260. Consultant-reviewed screening and review reports run about GBP 310–426. This is the closest international analogue to automated point and radius briefs.

### Cited Findings (Groundsure products page, fetched Nov 2026; all prices ex-VAT, "from" prices) — [Groundsure products](https://www.groundsure.com/products)
- Geo Insight (geological data report: BGS geology at 1:50k and 1:10k) from GBP 38.95 (about USD 51) — product card: [Geo Insight PDF](https://content.groundsure.com/hubfs/Product%20Cards/Geo-Insight-Product-Card.pdf).
- GeoRisk: residential from GBP 47.08, commercial from GBP 101.65. GeoRisk+: residential from GBP 61.53, commercial from GBP 112.35. GeoRisk Certificates from GBP 16.50.
- Metalliferous Mining Search from GBP 75. Stone Mining Search from GBP 75. CON29M Coal Mining Search from GBP 41.60 (residential, under 15 ha) or GBP 98.80 (commercial). Consultants Coal Mining Report from GBP 148.04. Cheshire Salt Search from GBP 34.65.
- Enviro Insight (for environmental consultants' desktop studies) from GBP 111.28. Map Insight from GBP 150.23. Groundsure Insight bundle (Enviro + Geo + Map) from GBP 199.19.
- Commercial environmental: Siteview from GBP 210, Screening from GBP 310, Review from GBP 425.60. Agricultural Search from GBP 345. Radon Check from GBP 3.50. UXO Search from GBP 175.
- Reseller prices are higher: Centremapslive lists Groundsure Residential from GBP 110 ex-VAT and Groundsure Insight at GBP 192 — [Centremapslive](https://blog.centremapslive.co.uk/groundsure-data-in-action-avoiding-risk-before-you-build).

### Inferences
- Automated point and radius briefs in mature markets are priced at about USD 50–260. Briefs with expert review are priced at about USD 400–550. A PakGeoLith 5 km point brief could follow a tiered model: an automated data brief at about PKR 10k–25k (USD 36–90), an expert-annotated brief at about PKR 50k–100k (USD 180–360), and a single-attribute "point-geology test" at about PKR 2k–5k (USD 7–18), comparable to Groundsure certificates and radon checks. These figures are inference, not sourced.
- In the UK, prices differ about 2x between residential and commercial products. That supports separate pricing for individual landowners and for companies or investors in Pakistan.

### Gaps
- No Landmark (UK) prices retrieved.
- No Australian or Canadian geological desktop study prices found.
- No Pakistani geotechnical or geological consultant desk-study rates found. PEC consultant rate schedules were not located.
