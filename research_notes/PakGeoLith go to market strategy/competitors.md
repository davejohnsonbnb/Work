# Competitive Landscape: Satellite/AI Mineral Exploration and Mineral-Data Platforms (Global + Pakistan) — relevance to PakGeoLith

Research date: 3 Nov 2026. About 20 searches and fetches. Most funding and launch facts date from 2024–2025. They are marked as such and may have changed since.

**Short answer to the founder's belief ("nobody in the world has this satellite system and we are the only ones offering this"):** The evidence does not support it.
- Satellite and AI mineral exploration is already a crowded, well-funded global category. Its leaders are valued at hundreds of millions to billions of USD.
- At least one of them, Fleet Space ExoSphere, has already been used in Pakistan, for Barrick at Reko Diq in 2024.
- Pakistan's own space agency launched a hyperspectral satellite (HS-1) in Oct 2025, partly for mineral mapping. SUPARCO already lists "Geology & Mineral Prospection" as a service.
- Free hyperspectral mineral maps (NASA EMIT) and free multispectral archives (Landsat, ASTER, Sentinel-2) cover arid regions like Balochistan.
- PakGeoLith may not have a unique satellite system, but its combined Pakistan-specific offer could still be distinctive (see Q4).

## Q1. Global satellite/remote-sensing and AI mineral exploration firms: what do they sell, and at what price?

### Takeaway
The global field is split into three groups:
- **Explorers that use AI to find deposits for themselves:** KoBold, Earth AI. These are not service vendors.
- **Exploration-tech vendors:** Fleet Space, VRIFY, GoldSpot, Farmonaut, Pixxel.
- **Raw satellite-data providers:** Planet Tanager, Pixxel, Wyvern.

Almost none publish prices. Everything is quote-based or per-km², which leaves room for PakGeoLith's transparent PKR price list. Fleet Space has already operated in Pakistan.

### Cited Findings
**KoBold Metals (USA): AI-driven explorer, investor-backed (Bill Gates and others)**
- Jan 2025: raised a $537M Series C at a $2.96B valuation, led by Durable Capital Partners and T. Rowe Price funds — [Mining Weekly](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/gates-backed-kobold-metals-raises-537m-in-funding-round-2025-01-06); [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/kobold-metals-ai-based-mining-company-raises-537-million-series-c-at-2-96-billion-valuation)
- Total raised is about $1.21B over 7 rounds per CB Insights. CB Insights lists a further Series C tranche of $280M (Nov 2025), and another aggregator reports $200M, so the reports conflict. A $4.17B secondary-market valuation figure is unverified — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/kobold-metals/financials); [Clay](https://clay.com/dossier/kobold-metals-funding)
- Business model: acquires and explores its own projects rather than selling reports, so it is not a direct service competitor.

**Earth AI (Australia/USA): vertically integrated AI explorer and driller**
- Jan 2025: raised a $20M Series B led by Tamarack Global and Cantos Ventures (Y Combinator also an investor). Funds go toward 50+ project sites and 100,000 m of drilling capacity — [Global Mining Review](https://www.globalminingreview.com/mining/31012025/earth-ai-raises-us20-million-for-ai-driven-mineral-exploration/); [Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/?p=1170737)
- Claims a 75% discovery success rate vs 0.5% in traditional exploration. This is a company claim, not independently verified. Total funding is about $41–43M — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/earth-ai/financials)
- Unlike vendors, it explores for its own account rather than selling software or services.

**Fleet Space Technologies / ExoSphere (Australia): the most directly relevant, and already active in Pakistan**
- Product: a LEO nanosatellite network, edge-computing seismic sensors (ambient noise tomography) and fast processing that produce 3D subsurface maps and AI drill targeting.
- July 2024: deployed ExoSphere at Barrick Gold's Reko Diq copper-gold project in Balochistan — [Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/barrick-taps-fleet-spaces-technology-to-advance-copper-exploration-at-reko-diq/); [Mining Technology](https://www.mining-technology.com/news/barrick-gold-uses-fleet-space-tech-for-reko-diq-exploration); [Australian Manufacturing](https://www.australianmanufacturing.com.au/fleet-space-deploys-exosphere-technology-to-pakistan/)
- Funding:
  - Dec 2024: A$150M Series D (USD $100M at a USD $525M valuation), led by Ontario Teachers' (Teachers' Venture Growth). In AUD the valuation is above A$800M — [OTPP](https://otpp.com/en-ca/about-us/news-and-insights/2024/fleet-space-closes-usd-100m-series-d-with-usd-525m-valuation/); [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/startupsmart/fleet-space-lands-150-million-valuation-800-million/)
  - May 2023: A$50M Series C — [InnovationAus](https://www.innovationaus.com/?p=47800)
- Customers named: Rio Tinto, Barrick, Gold Fields, Ma'aden. Opened a new HQ and hyperfactory in Adelaide in Sept/Oct 2025 — [AAP release](https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20250930ae87538)
- Pricing: not public (enterprise contracts).

**VRIFY / DORA (Canada): AI-assisted mineral discovery software (SaaS)**
- Feb 2025: raised a $12.5M Series B. Markets DORA as the "world's first AI-assisted mineral discovery platform" — [Newswire](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/vrify-lands-12-5m-series-b-to-advance-dora-the-world-s-first-ai-assisted-mineral-discovery-platform-828597303.html); [Canadian Mining Journal](https://canadianminingjournal.com/news/vrify-raises-12-5m-to-advance-the-worlds-first-ai-assisted-mineral-discovery-platform)
- Pricing model is a subscription that includes expert onboarding by geologists and data scientists, unlimited AI iterations and data updates. No dollar figures are published — [VRIFY pricing](https://vrify.com/pricing)
- Client counts vary by source: 26 (Sacra), about 30 (CMJ), "180+" (VRIFY marketing) — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/a9624d53-6744-49d8-9eeb-5f24807eabfd/); [VRIFY](https://vrify.com/abm/accelerating-koryx-copper-discoveries-with-ai)
- VRIFY's "world's first" marketing shows that other companies also claim to be first in this category.

**GoldSpot Discoveries (Canada): ML "Smart Targeting" plus geophysics services**
- Sells AI/ML prospectivity mapping, 3D modelling and drill targets to juniors and majors (Fortuna, Pretium, Critical Elements, Mosaic). In 2021 it acquired Ridgeline Exploration Services to add field geophysics — [Newsfile 2021 recap](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/119237); [Newsfile Mosaic](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/128720?lang=fr)
- These sources date from 2019–2022. I found nothing on "ProMIS", and the current corporate status is unverified.

**Farmonaut (India): a low-cost satellite mineral detection reports vendor, the closest analogue to PakGeoLith's product**
- Sells satellite mineral exploration reports quoted per project (by area, target mineral and tier). It offers two report tiers plus platform access. The "Premium" tier is pitched for drill planning and pre-feasibility scoping, with white-label and translation add-ons. It replies to quotes within one working day — [Farmonaut](https://farmonaut.com/mining/farmonaut.com/satellite-based-mineral-detection); [Farmonaut Mining Pro](https://farmonaut.com/?p=215433)
- Vendor claims (unverified): "90% accuracy on ranked target zones" and "80–85% lower exploration cost". Positions its reports as pre-JORC/NI 43-101 support rather than a resource estimate — same sources.
- Sells globally online and runs a mining affiliate program — [Farmonaut affiliate](https://farmonaut.com/mining/affiliate-success-stories-mining/affiliate-earned-big-with-the-farmonaut-mining-affiliate-program-real-case-study)
- It is an Indian firm, so selling into Pakistan has political and payment friction, but nothing technical stops it.

**Pixxel / Aurora (India): hyperspectral satellite operator and no-code analytics platform**
- Aurora is an AI geospatial platform for hyperspectral data. Mining applications include mineral detection, illegal-mining detection and compliance. Rio Tinto has been an early adopter since 2022. Pixxel has 50+ customers. It presented at a Geological Survey of India seminar on critical minerals — [Pixxel Aurora blog](https://www.pixxel.space/blogs/introducing-aurora-pixxels-earth-observation-studio); [Satellite Today](https://satellitetoday.com/imagery-and-sensing/2022/01/13/mining-group-rio-tinto-to-evaluate-pixxels-hyperspectral-satellite-imagery); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixxel)
- Pricing is reportedly per km² with a free trial. The source is a third-party summary, not official — [Lapaas](https://voice.lapaas.com/startup/pixxel/business-model/)

**Planet Labs (USA): Tanager hyperspectral constellation (VSWIR)**
- Planet markets Tanager for mineral-composition mapping. It released 50+ Tanager radiance datasets as open data (CC BY 4.0) — [Planet Pulse](https://www.planet.com/pulse/unleash-the-power-of-hyperspectral-over-50-tanager-radiance-datasets-now-available-on-planet-s/)
- Apr 30, 2026: agreed with Carbon Mapper on a SWIR-only Tanager (100 km swath, 30 m GSD) aimed in part at mineral exploration. Tanager-2 was shipped for launch in 2026 — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260430512259/en); [Planet IR](https://investors.planet.com/news/news-details/2026/Planet-Ships-Tanager-2-and-18-SuperDoves-to-Launch-Site/default.aspx)
- Other hyperspectral rivals: Pixxel (Firefly VNIR, Honeybee VNIR-SWIR planned) and Wyvern (5.3 m taskable VNIR) — [Geoawesome](https://geoawesome.com/planet-tanager-2-hyperspectral-earth-observation-business/)

**Fugro (Netherlands): large geo-services incumbent with Pakistan history**
- Jan 2017: €10M two-year contract with the Punjab Mineral Company (Punjab government) to plan and supervise iron-ore and other mineral exploration (geophysics, drilling, lab, resource estimate) — [GeoConnexion](https://geoconnexion.com/news/pakistan-government-chooses-fugro-for-extensive-minerals-exploration)

### Inferences
- Satellite-based mineral targeting is a mature global service category. Fleet Space, Farmonaut, Pixxel, VRIFY, GoldSpot and Planet all sell variants, so "only ones in the world" is false on its face. "Only Pakistan-focused, PKR-priced, self-serve provider" may be defensible but needs verification (see Q3).
- Farmonaut is the closest substitute: a cheap, online, quote-based satellite report with tiers, much like PakGeoLith's PKR 150k–1M reports (about USD 540–3,600 at roughly PKR 280/USD; the exchange rate is my assumption).
- Fleet Space is the high-end substitute for majors (Barrick at Reko Diq). PakGeoLith will not win Reko Diq-class work but can serve juniors, provincial leaseholders and small and medium miners whom Fleet Space ignores.
- Other names in the brief were not researched in this session: Minerva Intelligence, Descartes Labs, Orbital Insight, Satellogic, TerraCore, Exploration AI, Earth Genome, Mapbox, SRK, CSA Global. Mapbox and Orbital Insight are general geospatial tools, not mineral-exploration competitors. SRK and CSA are traditional consultancies that produce NI 43-101/JORC reports, which satellite reports cannot replace.

### Gaps
- No published prices exist for Fleet Space, VRIFY, GoldSpot, Pixxel or Farmonaut. All are quote-based, and I found no third-party price benchmarks for satellite alteration-mapping reports.
- No sources this session for Minerva Intelligence, Descartes Labs (largely pivoted away from mining per prior knowledge; unverified), TerraCore, Satellogic, Exploration AI or Earth Genome.
- The current status of GoldSpot after 2022 was not verified.

## Q2. Free/public data sources that substitute (global + Pakistan government)

### Takeaway
A technically capable buyer can get a lot for free: EMIT hyperspectral mineral maps, Landsat/ASTER/Sentinel archives, USGS–GSP digital geologic maps, the Punjab online cadastre and the KP cadastre portal. Pakistan's state is also building its own mineral data infrastructure: the SUPARCO HS-1 hyperspectral satellite, a National Minerals Data Centre and digitalised GSP data. That is the biggest long-term substitution risk, and also a potential partnership or data source.

### Cited Findings
**Global free data**
- NASA EMIT has produced global maps of 10 key surface minerals across arid dust-source regions, at 60 m resolution (L2B mineral ID plus uncertainty). Data is free via LP DAAC and NASA Earthdata, with Apache-2.0 tutorials on GitHub — [JPL](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasa-sensor-produces-first-global-maps-of-surface-minerals-in-arid-regions/); [data.gov](https://catalog.data.gov/harvest_record/2943537d-e13e-491a-b58f-83add4765f7e/raw). Coverage of Pakistan specifically was not confirmed, though Pakistan lies in the arid belt.
- Landsat scenes cost $400–4,000 before the archive opened in 2008 and are free now (vendor source) — [Farmonaut](https://farmonaut.com/?p=171882)
- ASTER outperforms Landsat for site-specific alteration mineral mapping (JICA Mauritania study) — [JICA](https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/11816691_03.pdf)
- USGS has published free ASTER alteration mapping for SE Afghanistan, next to Pakistan — [USGS OFR 2007-1006](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ofr20071006)
- Planet Tanager open datasets are available under CC BY 4.0 — [Planet](https://www.planet.com/pulse/unleash-the-power-of-hyperspectral-over-50-tanager-radiance-datasets-now-available-on-planet-s/)

**Pakistan government data and portals**
- **USGS–GSP digital geologic map of west-central Pakistan**: free, OFR 2011-1093 (Balochistan coal-basin synthesis) — [USGS](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ofr20111093)
- **GSP** publishes geological, geophysical and mineral maps. The 2022 catalogue PDF is online. GSP 1:50k sheets (2007–2024) are resold by East View — [GSP Catalogue 2022](https://gsp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Catalogue-2022.pdf); [East View](https://geospatial.com/country_profiles/pakistan/)
  - GSP has a Remote Sensing and GIS division.
  - In 2024 it tendered for firms to do 1:50k geological mapping and mineral investigation using remote sensing. This means the government buys RS mapping services, a possible PakGeoLith revenue channel — [GSP notice](https://gsp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Notice-1-1-24.pdf)
- **SUPARCO**:
  - Already lists "Geology & Mineral Prospection" among its remote sensing services — [SUPARCO](https://suparco.gov.pk/?p=3368)
  - Launched HS-1, Pakistan's first hyperspectral satellite, on 19 Oct 2025 from Jiuquan. Officials said it would let Pakistan "independently map its mineral reserves" — [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1950017/pakistan-launches-its-first-hyperspectral-satellite); [MOFA](https://mofa.gov.pk/press-releases/suparco-successfully-launches-pakistans-first-hyperspectral-satellite); [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/09/22/pakistan-to-launch-hyperspectral-satellite-in-october-to-boost-mineral-mapping-and-agriculture-research/)
  - Fleet: PRSS-1 (2018), EO-1 (Jan 2025), KS-1/remote sensing satellite (Jul 2025), HS-1 (Oct 2025) — [SUPARCO HS-1](https://suparco.gov.pk/major-programmes-satellite-programs/hs1.html)
- **Punjab Mines & Minerals Department**: online Mineral Cadastre System with a GIS of granted and vacant areas, online application and fee payment, an e-auction portal for mining rights and online production returns. Licensing also runs through e-Biz Punjab — [Punjab MMD](https://mnm.punjab.gov.pk/node/317); [e-Biz](https://mnm.punjab.gov.pk/node/355)
- **KP Minerals Development Department**: e-Government Mining Cadastre Portal (title registration and verification). Online applications and portfolio management were to start "after formal inauguration", so full live status is unclear — [KP portal](https://portal.kpminerals.gov.pk); [kpminerals.gov.pk](https://kpminerals.gov.pk)
- **Balochistan**: I found no online cadastre. The Balochistan Mines and Minerals Act 2025 and the 2002 Minerals Rules are published as PDFs — [Balochistan MMD](https://mmd.balochistan.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/balochistan-minerals-rules-2002.pdf)
- **National Minerals Data Centre (NMDC)**:
  - A GIS-based federal platform for online geospatial data and maps for investors — [Pakistan Observer](https://pakobserver.net/national-minerals-data-centre-being-es-tablished-for-investors-facilitation-projects-planning/)
  - Demonstrated at the Future Minerals Forum in Riyadh, Jan 2026 — [Profit Jan 2026](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/?p=218132)
- **PMIF 2025** (8–9 Apr 2025, Islamabad, under SIFC with OGDCL as organiser): 5,000+ delegates and 16 MoUs. Officials cited digitalisation of geological data and revitalisation of GSP — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/?p=192465); [OGDCL](https://ogdcl.com/node/99018)
- **Mineral production stats dashboard** from Pakistan Bureau of Statistics data, run privately by Gallup Pakistan Digital Analytics — [Gallup PK](https://galluppakistandigitalanalytics.com/energy-minerals-mining-statistics/)

### Inferences
- PakGeoLith's atlas and tenure layers largely repackage public data: GSP maps, the USGS–GSP map, the Punjab and KP cadastres and EMIT. Value has to come from integration, cleaning, a unified UI across provinces, Urdu/English and the AI assistant, not from owning data.
- Balochistan, the most important mineral province, appears to lack a public online cadastre. Assembling Balochistan tenure data is potentially the most defensible data asset.
- HS-1 plus the NMDC show that the state intends to provide hyperspectral mineral mapping itself. If SUPARCO sells or opens HS-1 data, it is either a cheap input for PakGeoLith or a direct state competitor. Claiming "nobody has this satellite system" is especially risky in Pakistan, because the government has just launched one and publicised it.

### Gaps
- Not confirmed: whether HS-1 data is commercially available, its price, or its operational status after launch.
- Not confirmed: whether the NMDC is publicly accessible as of Nov 2026, or what it contains.
- SIFC portal mineral-data contents and PMDC digital offerings were not found.
- Sindh and Gilgit-Baltistan cadastre status was not researched.
- EMIT coverage over Pakistan was not verified.

## Q3. Pakistan / South Asia players: consultancies, remote sensing, data startups, marketplaces, mining software

### Takeaway
I found no other Pakistani private startup offering satellite/AI mineral exploration as a productised service. The local competition is:
- state bodies (SUPARCO, GSP, PMDC, NMDC)
- small geoscience consultancies and Upwork/Fiverr freelancers selling GIS/RS mineral mapping
- foreign entrants (Fleet Space, Fugro, and the US Strategic Metals–FWO deal)

For gemstones, the government-linked PGJDC claims an online trading portal and gem exchanges. Trade itself is informal (Namak Mandi, Facebook/Instagram) and suffers from certificate fraud.

### Cited Findings
- I found no named Pakistani AI/satellite mineral exploration startup in searches. One unnamed listing references a company founded in 2021 focused on Pakistan's mineral riches — [a-leads](https://www.a-leads.co/company/95200529_4a43377e). This is a weak signal and needs follow-up.
- **GeoHaz Solutions**: a geoscience consultancy offering geological mapping, GIS, remote sensing and surveying. Its location and mineral focus are unverified — [GeoHaz](https://geohaz.odoo.com/about-us)
- **Freelancers**: many Pakistan-based geologists and GIS/RS specialists (Lahore, Islamabad) sell mineral mapping on Upwork and Fiverr. This is a low-cost substitute for PakGeoLith reports — [Upwork PK geologists](https://www.upwork.com/hire/geologists/pk/); [Upwork ArcGIS PK](https://www.upwork.com/hire/arcgis-consultants/pk/)
- **GSP** itself has an RS/GIS division and an Islamabad lab (GARL). PakGeoLith therefore competes with, and could also bid for, GSP tenders — [GSP](https://gsp.gov.pk)
- **Foreign entrants in Pakistan**:
  - Fleet Space at Reko Diq (2024), see Q1.
  - Fugro in Punjab (2017), see Q1.
  - US Strategic Metals: $500M MoU with the Frontier Works Organization on 8 Sept 2025 covering exploration, beneficiation and refining. The first shipment (antimony, copper concentrate, Nd/Pr) went out in Oct 2025. It is an MoU, not a binding contract — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2617851/); [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2025/9/25/strategic-handshake-how-pakistan-is-wooing-trump-with-critical-minerals); [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/09/08/pakistan-and-us-sign-mou-on-critical-minerals-exploration/amp/)
- Pakistan's mineral push continued through 2026, with France cooperation and a "critical minerals destination" framing — [ProPakistani Oct 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/10/16/pakistan-the-next-critical-minerals-destination/); [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40392189)
- **Gemstone marketplace landscape (Pakistan)**:
  - PGJDC (Pakistan Gem & Jewellery Development Company) says it has developed an online trading portal and runs gem exchanges in Quetta and Peshawar — [PGJDC](https://pgjdc.org/about.php?cId=72)
  - Peshawar's Namak Mandi has 700+ shops. Dealers photograph stones for online resellers, who add a margin — [GIA Gems & Gemology Summer 2021](https://www.gia.edu/gems-gemology/summer-2021-namak-mandi-a-pioneering-gemstone-market-in-pakistan); [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/pakistan/inside-peshawars-struggling-gemstone-industry-1203071)
  - A "Gemstone City" is planned in Peshawar with certification labs and export packaging. Its status is unverified — [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1090302)
  - Buyer forums report Pakistani Facebook and Instagram sellers reusing certificates copied from reputable dealers, which points to a trust gap — [Gem Society forum](https://forums.gemsociety.org/t/tourmalines-and-other-stones-from-pakistani-internet-sellers/8143)
  - Pakistani dealers already sell on Etsy and list in Mindat's dealer directory — [Etsy example](https://www.etsy.com/shop/OneStepGemstone); [Mindat dealer listing](https://www.mindat.org/dirclick_3285_Hammad+Gems.html)
- **India (South Asia comparator)**:
  - Pixxel is demonstrating to the Geological Survey of India — [Pixxel](https://www.pixxel.space/blogs/introducing-aurora-pixxels-earth-observation-studio)
  - Farmonaut sells satellite mineral reports — [Farmonaut](https://farmonaut.com/mining/farmonaut.com/satellite-based-mineral-detection)

### Inferences
- In Pakistan, the realistic competitors for PakGeoLith's survey reports are:
  1. freelance and small-consultancy GIS geologists, who are cheaper but not productised
  2. GSP and SUPARCO, which are free or subsidised for government but slow
  3. Farmonaut-style foreign online report vendors
  4. Fleet Space and Fugro, which serve majors and government only
- The marketplace faces incumbent informal networks (Namak Mandi, social media, Etsy) and a state portal (PGJDC). The clearest wedge is verified provenance and certification plus links to tenure records, which addresses the documented certificate-fraud problem.
- These global platforms were not researched this session and are listed for the report writer to treat as unverified context:
  - Commodity and mineral marketplaces: Alibaba, IndiaMART, Fastmarkets, Metal.com (SMM), Gemfields auctions
  - Mining data and intelligence: S&P Global Market Intelligence, Mining Data Online, Mindat
  
  Mindat, S&P Capital IQ Pro and Mining Data Online cover Pakistani occurrences and projects only thinly and in English, at enterprise prices.

### Gaps
- No Pakistani press coverage found of any rival "mineral-intelligence startup". Searches of Profit, Dawn and TechJuice by startup name were not exhaustive. This absence is a weak signal and should not be presented as proof.
- No coverage found of PakGeoLith itself in press.
- I found no data on whether PMDC sells data or services, or on SUPARCO's commercial pricing.
- I did not verify that the PGJDC online trading portal is live.
- Global marketplaces and mining-data subscriptions (Alibaba, IndiaMART, Fastmarkets, Metal.com, Gemfields, S&P, Mining Data Online, Mindat): no pricing or Pakistan-specific coverage verified this session.

## Q4. What is genuinely unique/defensible about PakGeoLith versus these?

### Takeaway
The satellite system itself is not unique. Global vendors, free NASA/USGS data and Pakistan's own HS-1 hyperspectral satellite all provide similar capability. What is plausibly distinctive is the **bundle and the localisation**:
- one Pakistan-wide geology, mineral and tenure atlas across provinces (including Balochistan, which has no public online cadastre)
- a mining-law register and licence-compliance reports tied to provincial rules
- an AI assistant over Pakistan-specific data
- transparent PKR pricing aimed at small and medium leaseholders
- a planned marketplace linked to verified tenure and provenance

### Cited Findings
- Competing global vendors publish no prices, and all are quote-based enterprise sales — [VRIFY pricing](https://vrify.com/pricing); [Farmonaut](https://farmonaut.com/mining/farmonaut.com/satellite-based-mineral-detection)
- Provincial tenure data is fragmented: Punjab has an online cadastre, KP has a partially launched portal, and I found no Balochistan portal. Each province administers its own mining law — [Punjab MMD](https://mnm.punjab.gov.pk/node/317); [KP portal](https://portal.kpminerals.gov.pk); [Lexology guide via RIAA Barker Gillette](https://riaabarkergillette.com/pk/insight-article/pakistan-mining-law-lexology-panoramic-guide/)
- Fleet Space already sells satellite-linked exploration in Pakistan. SUPARCO already lists geology and mineral prospection as a service and now runs a hyperspectral satellite — [Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/barrick-taps-fleet-spaces-technology-to-advance-copper-exploration-at-reko-diq/); [SUPARCO](https://suparco.gov.pk/?p=3368); [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1950017/pakistan-launches-its-first-hyperspectral-satellite)
- There is a documented trust and certification gap in the Pakistani gem trade — [Gem Society forum](https://forums.gemsociety.org/t/tourmalines-and-other-stones-from-pakistani-internet-sellers/8143); [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/pakistan/inside-peshawars-struggling-gemstone-industry-1203071)

### Inferences
- **Recommended reframing:** replace "nobody in the world has this" with something like "Pakistan's first integrated mineral-intelligence platform: geology, tenure, law and satellite targeting in one place, priced in PKR". The current claim can be disproven publicly with one link (Fleet Space at Reko Diq, or SUPARCO HS-1). That would damage credibility with sophisticated investors such as SIFC, Barrick and juniors.
- **Defensibility moats, ranked:**
  1. proprietary cleaned tenure and law dataset (especially Balochistan, Sindh and GB)
  2. ground-truth feedback loop from customers' field results
  3. local regulatory and compliance workflow
  4. government partnerships (an HS-1 data reseller or analytics partner with SUPARCO; bidding on GSP RS-mapping tenders)
  5. Urdu-language AI and the local sales channel
  
  The satellite analytics alone are commoditised (EMIT, ASTER and Sentinel are free; Farmonaut and Pixxel are cheap).
- **Threats:**
  - the NMDC or provincial portals becoming good enough and free
  - SUPARCO commercialising HS-1 products
  - Farmonaut/Pixxel-type vendors targeting Pakistani buyers online
  - local freelancers undercutting PKR 150k reports
- **Opportunity:** Pakistan's 2025–2026 critical-minerals push brings an influx of foreign investors needing quick, English-language Pakistan due diligence.
