# Government Partnership, JV and Institutional Routes in Pakistan for PakGeoLith

Research date: 3 Nov 2026. Each claim carries its source date where known. Search coverage of official .gov.pk pages was thin: sifc.gov.pk, gsp.gov.pk and ppra.org.pk returned no usable detail. Gaps are listed explicitly.

## 1. Federal bodies: SIFC, Petroleum Division / Mineral Wing, GSP, PMDC, SUPARCO. Roles and current initiatives

### Takeaway
Federal power in minerals runs through SIFC (convening, deal facilitation, fixing licensing logjams) and the Petroleum Division (policy, PMIF host, the minister who fronts deals). GSP and SUPARCO are the federal data owners and are in a capacity-building and digitisation phase that donors are funding. PMDC is the state miner signing JV-style MoUs, including the China-facing "Pak-China E-Mining Platform". No single federal geodata portal exists yet. That gap is PakGeoLith's opening.

### Cited Findings
- **SIFC role.** SIFC launched the National Minerals Harmonisation Framework in 2025 to bring the sector under one unified strategy. A federal minister cited "digitalisation of geological data, and revitalisation of the Geological Survey of Pakistan" as part of the same reform push — [search summary of CSIS / news, 2026](https://www.csis.org/analysis/minerals-diplomacy-meets-market-reality-case-pakistan)
- **SIFC as unblocker.** Through SIFC coordination, AJK reissued mining licences after a suspension that began in March 2024 (reported 20 May 2026) — [Radio Pakistan, 20-05-2026](https://www.radio.gov.pk/20-05-2026/sifcs-efforts-revitalize-mining-sector-in-pakistan)
- **SIFC and PMIF26.** SIFC endorses PMIF26, the Government of Pakistan organises it, and OGDCL is founding partner. It runs 12–13 Nov 2026 at the Jinnah Convention Centre, Islamabad, after being rescheduled from April. Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik gives the welcome address. OGDCL's Ahmed Hayat Lak is PMIF president. The technical conference has 50+ peer-reviewed papers, "geological survey data", "technology benchmarking" and a workshop on innovation in critical minerals mining. The previous edition drew about 5,000 delegates from 50+ countries — [ProPakistani, 28 Oct 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/10/28/pakistan-minerals-investment-forum-2026-pmif26-set-for-november-12-13-in-islamabad/); [Profit, 17 Oct 2026](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/10/17/pakistan-to-host-minerals-investment-forum-on-november-12-13-to-attract-foreign-investment); [pakminforum.com](https://www.pakminforum.com/)
- **Petroleum Division (Ministry of Energy).** Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik said in Riyadh in Jan 2026 that the government is working to reduce regulatory obstacles and harmonise mineral legal and policy frameworks — [Profit, 17 Jan 2026](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/01/17/pakistan-to-streamline-mineral-sector-regulations-petroleum-minister-says-in-riyadh/). He attended the Feb 2026 Critical Minerals Ministerial with US Secretary of State Rubio — [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/minerals-diplomacy-meets-market-reality-case-pakistan). Ministry site: [petroleum.gov.pk](https://petroleum.gov.pk/)
- **GSP digitisation.** The GSP Remote Sensing & GIS branch lists "digitization of geological maps" among its functions — [gsp.gov.pk](https://gsp.gov.pk/remote-sensing-gis/). A 1997-era project digitised GSP's national geological map, with layers for formations, structures, basins and metallogenic provinces — [ECIL project page](http://www.ecil.com/projects/details/Digital-Geological-Map-of-Pakistan)
- **GSP capacity, UK-funded.** A new UK–Pakistan partnership, the REMIT programme with International Geoscience Services (IGS), gives GSP staff hands-on training aligned to international standards (April–July 2026). Its aim is "a complete end to end digital survey process" — [24NewsHD, 16 Apr 2026](https://www.24newshd.tv/16-Apr-2026/new-uk-pakistan-partnership-will-bolster-pakistan-s-geological-survey); [IGS news](https://www.igsint.com/news/)
- **GSP and China.** GSP–China Geological Survey cooperation started with a 2008 MoU, renewed in 2013 and 2018. It produced geochemical anomaly maps for 69 elements — [GSP international collaboration](https://gsp.gov.pk/international-collaboration/); [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/national/pakistan-china-discuss-advancing-geoscientific-collaboration/)
- **Data fragmentation.** Pakistan's geological survey data is "outdated, fragmented, or insufficiently accessible to investors". A recent paper recommends a national digital geoscience database (maps, occurrences, exploration reports, borehole logs) and a National Geological Data Portal with tiered access, from open data to confidential project data — [ResearchGate paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/414308777_From_Uncertainty_to_Opportunity_Strengthening_the_Geological_Survey_Framework_for_Efficient_Mineral_Exploration_in_Pakistan). A 2026 ScienceDirect paper says Pakistan lacks a centralised, standardised mineral-resource repository, and its own compiled dataset is not public "due to institutional and data-sharing agreements" — [Ore Geology Reviews, 2026](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169136826001939)
- **Survey of Pakistan (separate from GSP).** Its plan is to digitise analogue archives into a georeferenced, searchable library and to build a national geoportal for cross-agency sharing — [Survey of Pakistan](https://www.surveyofpakistan.gov.pk/Detail/ZmU3MTg4NWYtNGRlYy00M2UyLWJkOTMtNDNhOTAwMDQ1Yjli)
- **PMDC.** At a CCCPK forum on 28 Jan 2026, Pakistan and China launched the "Pak-China E-Mining Platform" to share information and connect projects between Pakistani authorities and Chinese firms. The article does not name its developer. PMDC signed an MoU with POWERCHINA International and Pak China Investment Company covering investment facilitation, technical cooperation and joint development — [Asia News Network, 30 Jan 2026](https://asianews.network/pakistan-china-launch-e-mining-platform-sign-several-mous/). PMDC also met the GB Chief Secretary in July 2025 on the "next phase of mineral exploration" in GB — [PMDC PDF, Sept 2025](https://www.pmdc.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/MD-CS-GB-MEETING.pdf)
- **SUPARCO.** In Sept 2025 SUPARCO said it would launch a hyperspectral satellite in October 2025. Its stated uses include mineral exploration, agriculture, floods and glacier monitoring — [Profit, 22 Sep 2025](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/09/22/pakistan-to-launch-hyperspectral-satellite-in-october-to-boost-mineral-mapping-and-agriculture-research/). ARY separately reported a successful remote-sensing satellite launch from China — [ARY](https://arynews.tv/pakistan-suparco-remote-sensing-satellite-china/). SUPARCO signs sector MoUs with private firms, for example with E-Agri (Dec 2025) on space-driven agricultural intelligence — [ProPakistani, 19 Dec 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/12/19/suparco-and-e-agri-sign-landmark-mou-to-revolutionize-pakistans-agriculture-with-space-driven-intelligence/amp/)
- **Exploration gap.** Over 95% of Pakistan's mineral terrain is underexplored, and only two companies were actively exploring in 2025 — [CSIS, 2026](https://www.csis.org/analysis/minerals-diplomacy-meets-market-reality-case-pakistan)

### Inferences
- The SUPARCO–E-Agri MoU is the closest domestic template for a PakGeoLith–SUPARCO MoU on mineral and hyperspectral analytics. In that model a private firm adds value on state satellite data.
- GSP is being pushed toward an end-to-end digital workflow but has no investor-facing portal yet. PakGeoLith could offer one as a white-label portal or a tiered-access data platform (open, licensed, confidential), which mirrors the published recommendation.
- PMIF26 (12–13 Nov 2026) is the near-term venue to meet SIFC, the Petroleum Division, GSP, PMDC, OGDCL and provincial departments in one place. Its "technology benchmarking" and innovation workshop is the obvious slot to target.
- OGDCL, as PMIF founding partner and an active mineral entrant, is a potential anchor customer or co-venturer. This is an inference; the deal pipeline is unverified.
- The China-facing E-Mining Platform could make a competing state portal. Check who operates it before pitching a marketplace to PMDC.

### Gaps
- No source confirmed the current structure or mandate of a "Directorate General Mineral Wing" in the Petroleum Division, or of a "National Geoscience Data Centre" at GSP. Neither could be verified.
- Official sifc.gov.pk material on mining-specific programmes or startup facilitation was not retrieved.
- Whether SUPARCO's hyperspectral satellite (planned Oct 2025) actually launched and is operational is unconfirmed. So are its data-licensing terms.
- Who built or runs the Pak-China E-Mining Platform is unknown.

## 2. Provincial mines and minerals departments, cadastres and e-licensing

### Takeaway
Under the 18th Amendment, provinces own minerals. Each grants titles and collects royalties, so provincial departments are the real customers for cadastre, compliance and tenure tools. KP has a live online cadastre built on Trimble/Spatial Dimension Landfolio (FlexiCadastre). Punjab runs a PITB-built e-auction and a mineral cadastral portal. Balochistan's 2025 Act mandates an online cadastre but was suspended by the High Court in Feb 2026. GB is still proposing digital transformation.

### Cited Findings
- **KP.** The Minerals Development Department runs an e-Government Mining Cadastre Portal where title holders register, verify and validate titles and comment on title data — [portal.kpminerals.gov.pk](https://portal.kpminerals.gov.pk/Portal/). A Trimble testimonial from a KP Mines & Mineral Development Department official thanks "the members of the project from SAGE, Trimble and KP Minerals" — [Trimble landadmin](https://landadmin.trimble.com/tag/egovernment). The 2019 Landfolio user conference (Cape Town) included talks on "Pakistan Mining Cadastre" (Dr Hamid Ashraf) and a "Pakistan Mining Sector review" (Dr Sarfraz Ali) — [Trimble 2019 proceedings](https://landadmin.trimble.com/articles/2019-landfolio-for-natural-resources-user-conference-proceedings-cape-town/). Spatial Dimension's FlexiCadastre was renamed "Landfolio for natural resources", and Trimble later sold Spatial Dimension to acQuire Technology Solutions — [Trimble landfolio tag](https://landadmin.trimble.com/tag/landfolio)
- **KP law.** The KP Minerals Governance Act 2017 was passed 24 Oct 2017 and in force from 10 Nov 2017. It was amended in 2024 — [pakp.gov.pk Act PDF](https://www.pakp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/The-Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa-Minerals-Governance-Act-2017-Act-No.-XXXVI-2017.pdf); [Second Amendment 2024](https://www.pakp.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/The-KP-Mines-and-Minerals-Second-Amendment-Act2024.pdf). The KP Mines and Minerals Bill 2025 provides for a "legally binding online Mining Cadastre System" and was politically contested — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa_Mines_and_Minerals_Bill,_2025); [Geo explainer](https://www.geo.tv/latest/600247-what-is-kps-mining-bill)
- **Punjab.** The Mines & Minerals Department runs an E-Auction Portal for mining rights (online bidder registration and e-bidding), E-Biz Punjab single-window facilitation and a Mineral Cadastral Portal — [mnm.punjab.gov.pk](https://mnm.punjab.gov.pk/node/355). The Punjab IT Board (PITB) trained officials in Jan 2025 on the "newly introduced IT-based e-Auction System" — [PITB](https://pitb.gov.pk/node/10540)
- **Balochistan.** The Balochistan Mines and Minerals Act 2025 centralises licensing under a Licensing Authority in the DG Mines & Minerals and creates an online mining cadastre. In Feb 2026 the Balochistan High Court suspended the Act pending constitutional petitions alleging federal overreach — [CSIS, 2026](https://www.csis.org/analysis/minerals-diplomacy-meets-market-reality-case-pakistan). Department: [mmd.balochistan.gov.pk](https://mmd.balochistan.gov.pk/?p=108)
- **Gilgit-Baltistan.** The governing rules are the GB Mining Concession Rules 2016, amended 2024 — [Mondaq guide](https://www.mondaq.com/energy/1740300/mining-comparative-guide). The department proposes amending MCR-2016 and a "digital transformation" (automation, data analysis, real-time monitoring) to raise revenue, with no timeline given — [gbmines.gbit.gov.pk projects](https://gbmines.gbit.gov.pk/pages/projects). On 25 Oct 2026 GB CM Amjad Hussain Azar said mines and minerals are a provincial subject and "should not be the exclusive domain of the federal government". He also said GB has set up a PPP unit — [Express Tribune, 25 Oct 2026](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2636772/mines-minerals-provincial-subjects-says-g-b-cm?amp=1)
- **AJK.** Licensing was suspended from March 2024 and resumed through SIFC coordination (reported May 2026) — [Radio Pakistan](https://www.radio.gov.pk/20-05-2026/sifcs-efforts-revitalize-mining-sector-in-pakistan)
- **Harmonised framework (national template).** Each province is to set up a licensing authority in its mines department. The framework creates large-scale (above PKR 500m) and small-scale (PKR 25m–500m) titles, which go only to tax-registered companies. It also provides for district mining liaison committees, an appellate tribunal, a Mines and Minerals Force and a special court. Existing holders must comply within 18 months — [Norton Rose Fulbright, Apr 2025](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/2a6c8284/one-stop-shop-pakistan-unveils-harmonised-national-minerals-framework)
- **Academic precedent.** A paper in an Emerald journal sets out an implementation plan for a new mineral policy framework and mining cadastre system for Pakistan — [Emerald JSTPM](https://www.emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/JSTPM-10-2017-0052/full/html)

### Inferences
- In KP, PakGeoLith should complement Landfolio, not replace it, through value-added analytics, compliance reporting and investor-facing atlas layers fed by the cadastre. Replacing an incumbent system of record is a hard sell.
- Balochistan's cadastre mandate is frozen by litigation, so any build there carries legal risk until the BHC rules. A no-cost pilot or MoU is lower risk than a contract.
- Punjab works through PITB as its in-house IT builder. The route there is probably a PITB partnership or integration, not a direct department contract. This is inferred from PITB's role in the e-auction.
- The harmonised framework's 18-month compliance window for title holders creates demand for compliance-report and register products. Each province needs a cadastre and register under its new licensing authority.

### Gaps
- KP cadastre go-live date, contract value, funder (the World Bank was not confirmed) and the identity of local partner "SAGE" could not be verified.
- Sindh and AJK cadastre or e-licensing status was not found.
- Which provinces have actually enacted the harmonised Mines and Minerals Act 2025 by Nov 2026 is unconfirmed. KP and Balochistan versions were contested.

## 3. Policy frameworks and international deals: NMHF 2025, PMIF, US, Saudi, China, World Bank/ADB, EITI

### Takeaway
Policy momentum is real: the NMHF in April 2025, PMIF26 in Nov 2026, the US deals and Reko Diq financing. Execution is lagging. The US–FWO deal centres on a military entity, the Saudi Reko Diq stake is on hold, and China dominates current flows. Multilateral support is concentrated in Reko Diq project finance, not sector-wide data governance.

### Cited Findings
- **NMHF 2025.** Unveiled by Ishaq Dar at PMIF on 8–9 April 2025 in Islamabad. It replaces 6 regulatory frameworks, 8 legislative instruments and 36 sets of rules. It incorporated 694 of 750 proposed revisions toward a harmonised Mines and Minerals Act 2025 and a National Mineral Development Policy 2025. Royalties stay provincial — [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/2a6c8284/one-stop-shop-pakistan-unveils-harmonised-national-minerals-framework); [Profit, Mar 2025](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/03/28/pakistan-to-unveil-national-minerals-harmonisation-framework-2025-on-april-8-9/); [Business Recorder, 9 Apr 2025](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40356678). Pre-launch reports said all provinces, AJK and GB would adopt it by 26 Feb (2025). Adoption is unconfirmed.
- **Fiscal benchmark (NMHF).** The government take is about 76% against a six-country average of 68%. Company IRR is 14.5% against 17.5%, and the government targets about 18% via SEZ-like incentives — [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/2a6c8284/one-stop-shop-pakistan-unveils-harmonised-national-minerals-framework)
- **US–Pakistan.** US Strategic Metals (Missouri) signed an MoU with the Frontier Works Organization on 8 Sept 2025 at the PM House. It is described as about $500m and covers antimony, copper, gold, tungsten and REEs. Business Recorder noted that financial details were undisclosed — [Geo](https://www.geo.tv/latest/622638-pakistan-inks-mou-with-us-metal-company-on-critical-minerals); [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40381844); [ProPakistani, 8 Sep 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/09/08/pakistan-and-us-sign-mou-on-critical-minerals-exploration/amp/). A first shipment went in Oct 2025. CSIS calls the deal "not yet actionable" because USSM has not processed commercially — [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/minerals-diplomacy-meets-market-reality-case-pakistan). In Oct 2026 the government was urging Washington to lead investment in Balochistan minerals — [Express Tribune, 17 Oct 2026](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2635302/govt-seeks-us-investment-in-minerals)
- **Reko Diq financing.** The World Bank/IFC approved about $700m (June 2025) — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/world-bank-ifc-approve-700-million-loan-for-pakistan-s-reko-diq-project-125061300390_1.html). ADB provided $300m in loans and a $110m partial credit guarantee for Balochistan's equity (Aug 2025). JBIC lent $300m (Oct 2025) and US EXIM approved $1.25bn (Dec 2025). Production slipped from 2028 to 2029 after Barrick's Feb 2026 security review — [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/minerals-diplomacy-meets-market-reality-case-pakistan). Ownership is Barrick 50%, federal government 25%, Balochistan 25% — [Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/web/manara-may-invest-in-pakistans-reko-diq-mine-minister-says/)
- **World Bank technical assistance.** In July 2025 the World Bank was reported "likely" to provide no-cost technical assistance for an integrated infrastructure roadmap and socio-economic plan for Reko Diq and other Balochistan mining sites. Current status is unknown — [Profit, 10 Jul 2025](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/07/10/world-bank-likely-to-provide-technical-assistance-for-reko-diq-other-mining-sites-in-balochistan-report). The World Bank's Governance and Policy Project for Balochistan (P156411) targets sales tax and public financial management, not mining — [World Bank P156411](https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P156411)
- **Saudi.** Manara (Ma'aden–PIF JV) discussed a 10–20% stake from the federal government's 25% share for $500m–1bn (Jan 2025) — [Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/web/manara-may-invest-in-pakistans-reko-diq-mine-minister-says/). As of Jan 2026 the deal was "on hold" because Pakistan did not want to sell down. Pakistan was in talks with Saudi firms on other JVs — [AGBI, Jan 2026](https://www.agbi.com/mining/2026/01/pakistan-in-mining-project-talks-with-saudi-companies/). CSIS says Manara withdrew in 2025 — [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/minerals-diplomacy-meets-market-reality-case-pakistan). The sources conflict: "withdrew" versus "on hold".
- **China.** Chinese firms hold stakes in 4 of Pakistan's 6 active critical-minerals projects. In 2024 China took 95% of copper ore and concentrate exports and 98% of copper matte — [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/minerals-diplomacy-meets-market-reality-case-pakistan). The Pak-China E-Mining Platform and a PMDC–POWERCHINA MoU followed on 28 Jan 2026 — [ANN](https://asianews.network/pakistan-china-launch-e-mining-platform-sign-several-mous/)
- **Other.** Pakistan and France explored cooperation in minerals and mining — [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40392096). A think tank recommends institutionalising a Strategic Minerals Authority — [Jinnah Institute, 2026](https://jinnah-institute.org/?p=9294). Pakistan reportedly targets $8bn in mineral exports — [ProPakistani, 16 Oct 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/10/16/pakistan-targets-8-billion-mineral-exports/)

### Inferences
- Foreign entrants (US, Saudi, Chinese, French) need due-diligence-grade tenure, geology and compliance intelligence. PakGeoLith can position as the neutral, local data layer that SIFC offers in investor data rooms. A dedicated SIFC or PMIF investor data room is a plausible white-label product.
- Multilateral money is flowing into Reko Diq rather than sector-wide data systems. Donor-funded entry points are the UK REMIT/IGS capacity programme for GSP and possible future World Bank TA, which could subcontract local digital or data firms.

### Gaps
- Pakistan's EITI status could not be confirmed by search. Training knowledge suggests Pakistan is not an EITI implementing country, but this is unverified.
- No standalone, published national critical-minerals strategy document was found. Commentary says a coherent strategy is still lacking — [Open Magazine](https://openthemagazine.com/columns/inside-pakistans-ambitious-critical-minerals-gamble).
- No World Bank or ADB project focused specifically on KP or Balochistan mining governance or cadastre was found in 2025–26 results.

## 4. Procurement rules and startup / institutional programmes (PPRA, unsolicited proposals, PPP Authority, Ignite/NIC, PSEB, STZA)

### Takeaway
There is a formal route for unsolicited bids. Since a Jan 2020 cabinet amendment, PPRA rules let any private party submit an unsolicited proposal to a procuring agency, assessed by a committee of at least three technical experts (Rule 37-A as cited). The PPP Authority Act 2017, amended 2021, governs larger PPPs, and GB has its own PPP unit. Startup vehicles (Ignite/NICs, STZA) offer grants and tax incentives but no mining-specific procurement.

### Cited Findings
- **Unsolicited proposals.** The Federal Cabinet amended PPRA rules in Jan 2020. Any individual or agency, private or public, can give an unsolicited proposal to a procuring agency, to be assessed by a committee of at least three technical experts. A 2021 tender cites this as Rule 37-A — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/?p=526105); [ProPakistani, Mar 2022 (Pakistan Post ICT tender controversy)](https://propakistani.pk/2022/03/08/pakistan-posts-ict-digital-platform-project-tendering-process-raises-questions/)
- **PPP Authority.** The PPP Authority (Amendment) Act 2021 amended the 2017 Act's institutional framework, fund, viability gap fund and project development facility provisions — [UNEP LEAP / FAOLEX](https://leap.unep.org/en/countries/pk/national-legislation/public-private-partnership-authority-amendment-act-2021)
- **GB PPP unit.** It was set up by Oct 2026 — [Express Tribune, 25 Oct 2026](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2636772/mines-minerals-provincial-subjects-says-g-b-cm?amp=1)
- **Ignite.** Ignite is a government-owned non-profit company under MoITT. It gives grants to startups, has established 8 National Incubation Centers plus specialised incubators, and has a business plan for 2024–28 — [Ignite Business Plan 2024–28](https://ignite.org.pk/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/Ignite-Business-Plan-2024-28-Final-Version.pdf); [Briter](https://www.briter.co/companies/ignite-national-technology-fund)
- **STZA.** The Special Technology Zones Authority Act 2021 (under Cabinet Division) gives zone enterprises 10-year incentives from licence date, property tax exemption, and dividend and capital-gains exemptions for venture funds investing in STZs — [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40107930); [STZA FAQs](https://www.stza.gov.pk/storage/2023/09/FAQs-for-Zones-ZDs-and-ZEs.pdf); [stza.gov.pk](https://www.stza.gov.pk/)

### Inferences
- **Practical sequence.**
  1. Non-binding MoU or pilot at no cost with GSP, SUPARCO or a provincial department. Precedent: the SUPARCO–E-Agri MoU.
  2. A PPRA Rule 37-A unsolicited proposal for a defined deliverable, such as an investor data room, compliance-report module or digitisation of legacy reports.
  3. Competitive tender, where PakGeoLith should expect its unsolicited proposal to be competed. The Pakistan Post ICT case shows the risk of perceived favouritism.
- Provincial projects fall under the provincial PPRA rules (Punjab, KP, Sindh, Balochistan), not federal PPRA.
- Locating in an STZ, or being a PSEB-registered IT exporter, can cut tax on export revenue, for example from foreign investor subscriptions. This is an inference; PSEB terms were not verified.

### Gaps
- Exact current text and thresholds of PPRA Rule 37-A, provincial unsolicited-proposal rules, and PPP Authority unsolicited-proposal provisions were not retrieved.
- PSEB registration benefits and any SIFC startup-facilitation desk were not verified.
- No precedent was found of a Pakistani startup winning a mining-sector government data contract.

## 5. Global precedents and partnership models

### Takeaway
Governments commonly partner private geodata and tech firms through several structures: equity plus 50/50 exploration JVs (Ma'aden–Ivanhoe Electric), state-paid national airborne surveys with data sold to investors (Zambia–Xcalibur), and donor-funded cadastre vendors (Spatial Dimension/Landfolio in KP, Angola and Botswana). Resource-for-data deals also occur, such as KoBold's DRC deal to digitise historical data.

### Cited Findings
- **Saudi Arabia.** Ma'aden bought 9.9% of Ivanhoe Electric for $126.5m and formed a 50/50 exploration JV over at least 48,500 km² (copper, nickel, gold, silver). The JV uses the Typhoon geophysical system and the Computational Geosciences data-analytics subsidiary. It was finalised in May 2023 and closed in July 2023 — [Global Mining Review](https://www.globalminingreview.com/mining/16052023/maaden-and-ivanhoe-to-explore-untapped-resources-in-saudi-arabia/); [Kitco, Jul 2023](https://www.kitco.com/news/article/2023-07-06/ivanhoe-electric-establishes-exploration-jv-saudi-arabian-mining-giant); [PIF](https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/news-and-insights/newswire/2023/maaden-finalizes-deal-to-explore-saudi-arabias-key-mining-resources/)
- **Zambia.** The government contracted Canadian Xcalibur for a nationwide high-resolution airborne magnetic, radiometric and gravity survey covering about 2.7m line-km. It was 42.6% complete by 15 Oct 2025, with data acquisition due by about July 2026. Data will be made available to investors and the public "for a nominal fee" — [Zambia Monitor](https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambias-nationwide-geological-survey-on-track-for-2026-completion-mines-ministry-confirms/); [ITWeb Africa](https://itweb.africa/article/zambia-adopts-geological-mapping-tech-in-minerals-exploration/JBwEr7n36EjM6Db2)
- **DRC.** KoBold Metals' 2025 agreement with Congo covers digitising historical geological data and applying AI-driven targeting — [Geomechanics.io](https://www.geomechanics.io/news/article/congobelgium-transfer-of-colonial-geological-records-key-points-for-mine-planners)

### Inferences
Candidate partnership models for PakGeoLith, with the nearest precedent for each:
1. **MoU or pilot (no cost).** With GSP, SUPARCO or a province, for example digitising one district's legacy reports or a hyperspectral pilot. Precedent: SUPARCO–E-Agri.
2. **Data licensing / revenue share.** PakGeoLith digitises GSP or provincial archives and sells tiered access, sharing revenue with the state as data owner. Precedents: Zambia's "nominal fee" model and DRC/KoBold digitisation for access.
3. **White-label portal / investor data room.** For SIFC, PMIF or a provincial licensing authority, alongside existing cadastres (Landfolio in KP, PITB in Punjab).
4. **PPP (PPP Authority or provincial PPP units such as GB's).** For a national geoscience data platform under a build-operate-transfer or service-fee model.
5. **JV with a state miner or SOE (PMDC, OGDCL).** Exploration-targeting JVs on the Ma'aden–Ivanhoe model, with PakGeoLith providing AI and satellite targeting for equity or carried interest.
6. **Subcontractor to donor programmes.** UK REMIT/IGS, possible World Bank TA, or large cadastre vendors needing local data partners.

Comparator search attempted, not resolved: no source was found linking the Saudi Geological Survey to Earth AI, nor details of the Saudi National Geological Database. Kenya airborne survey, Mozambique cadastre, Morocco and Chile precedents were not researched within budget.

### Gaps
- Contract values, IP and data-ownership clauses of the Zambia–Xcalibur and KoBold–DRC deals were not found.
- No retrieved precedent of a small or startup geodata firm (as opposed to large vendors) winning a government data deal.

## 6. Risks: data sovereignty, security, 18th Amendment jurisdiction, military-linked entities

### Takeaway
The biggest structural risks are federal–provincial jurisdiction fights (the BHC suspension of the Balochistan Act in Feb 2026; GB's CM asserting provincial control in Oct 2026), security in Balochistan, and the dominance of military-linked entities (FWO) in flagship deals. Data-sharing restrictions already limit access to geodata.

### Cited Findings
- **Jurisdiction.** The 18th Amendment (2010) gave provinces exclusive jurisdiction over minerals. Federal control remains over nuclear minerals and oil and gas under the 1948 Act. The NMHF requires provincial adoption. The BHC suspended Balochistan's 2025 Act in Feb 2026 over alleged federal overreach — [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/minerals-diplomacy-meets-market-reality-case-pakistan). The GB CM said on 25 Oct 2026 that minerals should not be the federal government's exclusive domain — [Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2636772/mines-minerals-provincial-subjects-says-g-b-cm?amp=1)
- **Military-linked entities.** FWO, the army's engineering branch, is the counterparty to the USSM deal — [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/minerals-diplomacy-meets-market-reality-case-pakistan). Rare Earth Exchanges cautions that FWO is "primarily a military engineering organization" whose mining footprint varies by province — [Rare Earth Exchanges](https://rareearthexchanges.com/?p=11200). Commentary on converting the MoU to cash flow — [The News](https://www.thenews.com.pk/print/1377386-comment-converting-500m-fwo-ussm-mou-into-cash-flow). The NMHF also proposes a Mines and Minerals Force — [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/2a6c8284/one-stop-shop-pakistan-unveils-harmonised-national-minerals-framework)
- **Security.** In late Jan 2026, BLA attacks killed 36 civilians and 22 security personnel. A 2024 Duki coal mine attack killed 21 miners and led 40% of local mines to close. Balochistan had more than 50% of Pakistan's attacks in 2025 with 6% of the population — [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/minerals-diplomacy-meets-market-reality-case-pakistan)
- **Governance and legal.** Pakistan ranks 136/182 on TI's 2025 index. The court backlog is 2.4m cases. There is no US–Pakistan BIT. The Reko Diq ICSID award (about $6bn, 2019) is a reminder of contract risk — [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/minerals-diplomacy-meets-market-reality-case-pakistan); [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/1525616)
- **Data sovereignty.** Researchers report that mineral datasets are not publicly shareable "due to institutional and data-sharing agreements" — [ScienceDirect 2026](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169136826001939). The proposed GSP portal includes a confidential tier — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/414308777_From_Uncertainty_to_Opportunity_Strengthening_the_Geological_Survey_Framework_for_Efficient_Mineral_Exploration_in_Pakistan)

### Inferences
- Contract with each province separately and keep federal (SIFC/GSP) relationships as convening and data-sourcing channels. A federal-only deal could be challenged as overreach.
- Expect security vetting and possibly data-localisation demands (Pakistani hosting) for satellite and geoscience data, especially near borders, CPEC corridors and strategic or nuclear minerals. Design for on-premises or sovereign-cloud hosting and role-based access.
- Treat FWO, NLC and Fauji-linked firms as potential customers or partners, not competitors. Relationships with military-linked entities can open doors but may raise reputational and sanctions-adjacent concerns for Western investors.

### Gaps
- No source was found on formal security-clearance or data-localisation rules for geodata or satellite imagery in Pakistan (for example a Survey of Pakistan mapping-permit regime or the Personal Data Protection Bill status).
- The mining footprint of NLC and the Fauji Foundation was not researched.
