# Marketing Channels, Events, Associations and GTM Tactics for PakGeoLith

Scope: Pakistan mineral-intelligence startup (geology/tenure atlas, AI assistant, satellite exploration reports, compliance reports, mining-law register, subscriptions, planned mineral & gemstone marketplace; English/Urdu). Current as of 3 Nov 2026. Research used 16 search/fetch calls. Several items could not be verified and are listed under Gaps rather than stated as fact.

## 1. Pakistani mining associations, chambers, government bodies and universities

### Takeaway
The confirmed partner targets are a few named trade bodies (Frontier Mines Owners Association in KP, All Pakistan Marble Industries Association, Sarhad Chamber), the state companies (PGJDC, PASDEC, PMDC, OGDC) and the universities that are PMIF26 academic partners (NUST, University of Peshawar, UET Peshawar, UET Lahore). Most association sources are old (2014–2018), so current officeholders need fresh checks. Petroleum geoscience bodies (PAPG, SPE Pakistan) are a secondary channel for reaching geoscientists.

### Cited Findings
- **Frontier Mines Owners Association (KP):** a member said about 600 marble units operate on Warsak Road, Peshawar, and that the industry employs about 1 million people directly or indirectly. President Sher Bandi Marwat has blamed weak technology, little professional training and heavy taxes for holding back KP mining and gemstones. — [APP feature: KP's marble](https://www.app.com.pk/features/kps-marble-a-treasure-for-economic-prosperity/)
- **KP marble:** an industry official reported about 4,300 marble processing units in KP, of which about 1,500 operate in government-approved industrial zones. — [APP feature](https://www.app.com.pk/features/kps-marble-a-treasure-for-economic-prosperity/)
- **All Pakistan Marble Industries Association (APMIA):** its chairman said Balochistan provides up to 90% of Pakistan's roughly $20M a year in marble exports. He also said lease transfers there are hard because mountains are treated as government property. This tenure pain point maps directly to PakGeoLith's tenure atlas and compliance products. — [Dawn (older article)](https://www.dawn.com/news/86894)
- **APMIA and Balochistan licence fees:** APMIA has been named in opposition to Balochistan's licence-fee increase, from about Rs10,000–0.2M to Rs0.5–2M. A regulatory-change alert service would fit this. — [Dawn](https://dawn.com/news/387489) (date of the notification is not clear in the snippet)
- **Sarhad Chamber of Commerce and Industry** signed an MoU with APMIA to promote the marble sector, which shows chambers co-sign sector initiatives. — [Pakistan Observer](https://pakobserver.net/?p=54794)
- **Pakistan Stone Development Company (PASDEC)** signed an MoU with the Government of Balochistan in 2018 to develop marble and granite. — [PID press release](https://pid.gov.pk/site/press_detail/8294)
- **Naming caveat:** sources use inconsistent names for the marble association (for example "All Pakistan Marble Mining Processing Industry and Exporters Association"). — [search synthesis across stonenews.eu and Dawn](https://stonenews.eu/?p=42215)
- **PGJDC (Pakistan Gems & Jewellery Development Company):** its site lists regular gem exhibitions in Islamabad, Peshawar, Quetta, Lahore and Gilgit. The most recent dated PGJDC event found was a Karachi exhibition on 14–15 June 2025. — [PGJDC Gem Exhibitions](https://pgjdc.org/company-initiatives.php?catId=27); [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1009762)
- **PMIF26 academic partners:** NAVTTC, NUST, University of Peshawar, and the engineering universities of Peshawar and Lahore. — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/10/28/pakistan-minerals-investment-forum-2026-pmif26-set-for-november-12-13-in-islamabad/)
- **PMIF26 exhibitors:** PMDC, NRL, FWO, Barrick, PPL, GHPL, Reko Diq and PMPL. — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/10/28/pakistan-minerals-investment-forum-2026-pmif26-set-for-november-12-13-in-islamabad/)
- **PAPG:** affiliated with AAPG and formed in Karachi in 1994. Its Annual Technical Conference (ATC) has historically been run jointly with SPE Pakistan. — [Search and Discovery / AAPG PAPG collection](https://www.searchanddiscovery.com/specialcollections/papg.htm)
- **ATC 2026 (31st):** held in Islamabad at the Serena Hotel on 28–29 Jan 2026, per Mari Energies. — [Mari Energies](https://www.marienergies.com.pk/insight/marienergies-at-atc-2026). A LinkedIn post gives 2–4 Feb 2026 and credits SPE Pakistan as host, so the sources conflict. — [LinkedIn](https://pk.linkedin.com/in/sayedhasnainshah)
- **Bahria University Karachi** has hosted PAPG conference activity before, so its geology students and faculty are a reachable channel. — [Bahria University](https://bahria.edu.pk/bukc/?p=10032)

### Inferences
- APMIA and the Frontier Mines Owners Association are the strongest first partners because they speak for thousands of small units with tenure and licensing pain. A free "licence/lease status check" offered through them would address a stated grievance.
- University geology departments (Peshawar, Punjab, NUST, UET, Bahria) can act as both credibility partners and a source of field-agent or ambassador talent. Being a PMIF academic partner shows they sit inside the government ecosystem.
- PGJDC's multi-city exhibitions are a low-cost way to reach gem traders before the marketplace launches.

### Gaps
- I could not verify current websites, leadership or membership counts for: the Pakistan Gems & Jewellery Traders & Exporters Association, Balochistan and Punjab mine owners' associations, the FPCCI standing committee on mines and minerals, the Pakistan Society of Mining Engineers, or the Geological Society of Pakistan. They need direct checks.
- I did not search the National Centre of Excellence in Geology (Peshawar) or the geology departments at Punjab, Karachi, Balochistan and Quaid-i-Azam universities individually.
- PGJDC's 2026–27 exhibition calendar was not found.

## 2. Events calendar (2026–2027)

### Takeaway
The closest high-value event is PMIF26 in Islamabad on 12–13 Nov 2026, about nine days after this report, so any booth or side-event push must start now. Internationally, the 2027 sequence is: Future Minerals Forum, Riyadh (12–14 Jan); Tucson (late Jan to mid Feb); Mining Indaba, Cape Town (8–11 Feb); Hong Kong Diamond, Gem & Pearl Show (around 2–6 Mar); PDAC, Toronto (7–10 Mar).

### Cited Findings
- **PMIF26 (Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026):**
  - Dates and venue: 12–13 Nov 2026, Jinnah Convention Centre, Islamabad.
  - Backing: government-run and endorsed by SIFC. OGDC is founding partner and Wood Mackenzie is lead conference producer.
  - Strategic partners: PPL, Mari, GHPL, Reko Diq, PMPL, FWO. Knowledge partners: Milbank, White & Case, Lazard.
  - Speakers include PM Shehbaz Sharif, Field Marshal Asim Munir, John Thornton (Barrick) and Robert Friedland (Ivanhoe).
  - Programme: a two-day strategic conference, a technical conference with more than 50 peer-reviewed papers, a critical-minerals innovation workshop, a Mineral Expo, procurement briefings and a European Pavilion.
  - The 2025 edition drew about 5,000 delegates from more than 50 countries and produced 16 MoUs.
  — [ProPakistani, 28 Oct 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/10/28/pakistan-minerals-investment-forum-2026-pmif26-set-for-november-12-13-in-islamabad/)
- **PMIF26 reschedule:** the forum was originally set for 8–9 Apr 2026 and was moved in March 2026, citing regional developments and travel uncertainty. — [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1179098); [The News](https://www.thenews.pk/print/1404853-govt-reschedules-minerals-investment-forum-2026-to-november)
- **Envoy and China outreach:** the petroleum minister asked Pakistani envoys to mobilise global participation and invited Chinese firms. — [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1143723); [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1152429)

- **First international gemstone exhibition in Pakistan:** the PM's office announced it for July 2026, with no city or organiser named. — [Arab News Pakistan, 27 Apr 2026](https://www.arabnews.pk/pakistan/pakistan-to-host-first-international-gemstone-exhibition-in-july-to-unlock-untapped-potential-2641456)
- **Gem-sector context from the same article:**

  2. Future Minerals Forum, Riyadh, to reach Gulf investors. Saudi Maaden already buys AI exploration services (see section 4).
  3. PDAC, to reach junior explorers and the diaspora in Canada.
  4. Tucson and Hong Kong, as marketplace and gem-buyer channels.
- Because PMIF26 was rescheduled once, keep event plans flexible.

## 3. Digital channels in Pakistan

### Takeaway
YouTube (54.3M ad reach) and Facebook (52.9M) are the mass-reach channels. TikTok's 18+ ad reach is listed at 79.9M, but that figure equals the total social-identity figure and may be an artefact. LinkedIn (18M, up 28.6% year on year) is the professional channel. Facebook's Pakistani audience is about 75% male, which suits a male-dominated mine-owner and gem-trader audience.

### Cited Findings
- **Pakistan digital audience, DataReportal Digital 2026 (data around Oct 2025):**
  - Internet users: 117M (45.6% penetration).
  - Social media identities: 79.9M (31.2%).
  - Mobile connections: 194M.
  - Ad reach: YouTube 54.3M (down 2.9%), Facebook 52.9M (up 15.5%), Instagram 22.4M, LinkedIn 18.0M (up 28.6%), TikTok 18+ 79.9M.
  — [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan)
- **NapoleonCat estimates:**
  - Facebook: about 71.2M users in June 2026, 75.4% male. Later months show lower figures (about 69.4M in Aug, 66.0M in Sep), so the series is volatile.
  - Messenger: about 53.7M (Aug 2026).
  - LinkedIn: about 15.1M (June 2026).
  — [NapoleonCat Facebook](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/facebook-users-in-pakistan/2026/06/); [NapoleonCat social](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/social-media-users-in-pakistan/2026/)

### Inferences
- Run Urdu and Pashto YouTube explainers, for example "how to check a mining lease", "how to read a geology map" and "ML/EL application steps per province". Pair them with Facebook and Meta lead ads that lead into WhatsApp click-to-chat. This fits an audience that is male-skewed and mobile-first.
- Use LinkedIn for investor, consultant and diaspora outreach, given its fast growth.
- SEO in English and Urdu for "mining lease [province]", "gemstone price Pakistan" and mineral-plus-district queries is plausible because there are few local information competitors. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- No WhatsApp user statistics for Pakistan were found (DataReportal's free page did not show them).
- Specific Facebook or WhatsApp groups for mine owners and gem traders, with member counts, were not identified.
- Google Ads CPC benchmarks for Pakistan B2B were not researched.

## 4. B2B geo/mining-tech customer acquisition examples

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin. Fleet Space is the best-documented case: event-centred marketing at PDAC (product launches plus an "ExoSphere & Beer" side event), customer co-presentations, equity-for-services deals with juniors (Thor Energy), and a local JV with a Saudi partner (Tahreez) to win a multi-year Maaden contract. VRIFY, Earth AI, GoldSpot, Landfolio and DigBee were not documented in this research.

### Cited Findings
- **Fleet Space customer base:** ExoSphere secured more than 30 customers after launching commercially in March 2022, including Rio Tinto, Barrick, Core Lithium and Gold Fields. — [SpaceNews](https://spacenews.com/?p=159446). A later figure is more than 40 explorers. — [Mining Technology](https://www.mining-technology.com/featured-company/2025-fleet-space/)
- **Fleet at PDAC 2024:** Fleet used PDAC to announce new ExoSphere features and hosted an "ExoSphere & Beer" side event where client explorers IsoEnergy and Power Nickel presented. — [Fleet Space](https://fleetspace.com/news/pdac-2024-fleet-spaces-view-from-the-heights-of-mineral-exploration)
- **Thor Energy deal:** Fleet earns an equity interest in junior Thor Energy through a strategic share subscription tied to ANT surveys at Alford East. — [Asia Pacific Defence Reporter](https://asiapacificdefencereporter.com/posts/fleet-space-technologies-takes-stake-in-thor-energy); [Global Mining Review](https://www.globalminingreview.com/mining/05092023/fleet-space-and-thor-energy-partner-to-advance-mineral-exploration/amp/)
- **Maaden contract:** Maaden signed a four-year contract with the Fleet Space–Tahreez JV covering up to 12,012.6 km² of the Arabian Shield. — [Global Mining Review, May 2025](https://www.globalminingreview.com/technology-digitalisation/06052025/maaden-signs-fleet-space-tahreez-jv-for-multi-year-exploration-contract/); [Alturki Holding](https://alturkiholding.com/en/our-news/fleet-space-tahreez-form-jv-to-accelerate-ksa-s-leadership-in-ai-powered-mineral-exploration/)
- **VRIFY:** CB Insights lists only about 5 known customers (for example RUA Gold and Southern Cross Gold) plus a Kodiak testimonial on VRIFY AI. This is an aggregator figure. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/fleet-space-technologies-vs-vrify)

### Inferences
- Models PakGeoLith could copy:
  - A PMIF or PDAC side event where customers present their findings.
  - A local JV or partnership with a well-connected Pakistani firm. This mirrors Fleet–Tahreez, for example with an FWO- or OGDC-linked contractor, or a consultancy.
  - Reduced-fee reports for small leaseholders in exchange for a revenue share or equity, mirroring Fleet–Thor.
  - Customer case studies as the core trust content.
- Field-agent and referral-commission networks are not documented for these companies. Treat them as a local adaptation, since informal brokers and munshis already work in Pakistani mining. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- No sourced information was found on GTM for Earth AI, GoldSpot, Landfolio (Spatial Dimension/Trimble) or DigBee, on VRIFY's freemium, webinar or partnership tactics, or on conversion metrics. A follow-up search is needed.

## 5. Diaspora and Gulf investor channels

### Takeaway
Overseas Pakistanis are a large, digitally reachable pool of capital. Roshan Digital Account inflows reached about $14.16B cumulatively by Sep 2026, and the scheme opened to foreign nationals and companies in March 2026. Gulf demand is shown by Saudi buyers of mining tech and by PMIF's government-to-government framing.

### Cited Findings
- **RDA inflows:** cumulative $14.157B by Sep 2026, with monthly inflows of about $250–280M in Jul–Sep 2026. — [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1291269); [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1263824)
- **RDA usage:** as of June 2026, $8.44B had been used locally and $2.09B repatriated. — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/07/10/roshan-digital-account-gross-inflows-reach-dollar1337-billion-by-june-2026)
- **RDA expansion:** the scheme was opened to foreign nationals, companies and institutional investors in March 2026. — [search synthesis of APP RDA coverage](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1194171) (verify the exact article)
- **Remittances:** a record $38.3B in FY2025. The SBP projected $42B for FY2026, which is a projection. — [Digital Pakistan](https://digitalpakistan.pk/pakistan-remittances-2025-the-38-billion-lifeline-explained-simply/); [Daily Pakistan](https://en.dailypakistan.com.pk/19-Feb-2026/overseas-pakistanis-send-12b-through-roshan-digital-accounts-sbp)
- **Gulf and US outreach:** the government is seeking US investment in Balochistan minerals and has mineral agreements with the US, Saudi Arabia and China. — [Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2635302/govt-seeks-us-investment-in-minerals); [Arab News Pakistan](https://www.arabnews.pk/pakistan/pakistan-to-host-first-international-gemstone-exhibition-in-july-to-unlock-untapped-potential-2641456)

### Inferences
- Offer diaspora investors a "remote due-diligence pack" in English: a tenure check, a satellite survey and a compliance summary. Target the UK, Canada (around PDAC), the Gulf (around FMF) and the US. LinkedIn and community associations are the likely channels.
- Embassies and commercial attachés are already mobilising PMIF participation, so they could co-promote.

### Gaps
- Specific diaspora investor networks (for example OPEN Silicon Valley, Pakistani business councils in Dubai and Riyadh) were not researched.
- No data was found on how much diaspora money flows into mining specifically.

## 6. Brand/trust issues and credibility signals

### Takeaway
I found no Pakistan-specific press reports of mining-lease title fraud or gem-investor scams. The closest items are a PKR 1B gold-fraud arrest and a fake investment company in Gilgit-Baltistan that took over Rs240M from about 700 people. The trust problem is real but needs better sourcing. Fleet's use of blue-chip client names (Rio Tinto, Barrick) shows the value of reference customers.

### Cited Findings
- **Gilgit-Baltistan fake investment company:** a fake company took over Rs240M from 700 people in GB. Many victims were retired government officers, and the company had no licence. — [Profit / Pakistan Today](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/?p=42866)
- **Gold fraud:** Pakistan's major gold-fraud case is worth PKR 1B, and suspect Sheikh Waseem was detained in Islamabad. — [Nukta](https://nukta.com/police-arrest-gold-fraudster)
- **Lease-transfer confusion:** confusion over lease transfer in Balochistan (mountains treated as state property) creates title uncertainty. — [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/86894)
- **Credibility partners in the PMIF26 ecosystem:**
  - International law firms and banks as knowledge partners: Milbank, White & Case, Lazard.
  - BGR (Germany's Federal Institute for Geosciences and Natural Resources) and the World Bank as speakers.
  — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/10/28/pakistan-minerals-investment-forum-2026-pmif26-set-for-november-12-13-in-islamabad/)
- **Reference customers:** Fleet markets itself through named majors (Rio Tinto, Barrick, Gold Fields). — [SpaceNews](https://spacenews.com/?p=159446)

### Inferences
- Credibility signals to build:
  - An advisory board with university geology faculty (Peshawar, NUST, UET) and a retired GSP or PMDC official.
  - A partnership with a JORC/NI 43-101 Competent Person or Qualified Person firm, so reports can be upgraded for international investors.
  - ISO 27001 for data handling, and a clear disclaimer that satellite reports are not resource estimates.
  - Data sourced from official provincial mining cadastres with timestamps.
  - The marketplace should require verified lease documents and escrow to separate PakGeoLith from scam-prone channels.

### Gaps
- No sourced reporting was found on fake mining leases or title fraud in Pakistan, nor on gemstone-certification fraud. A targeted search of Dawn, Express Tribune and FIA/NAB archives is needed.
- Whether provincial mining cadastres (for example KP's online mineral title system) provide public APIs or data was not researched here.
